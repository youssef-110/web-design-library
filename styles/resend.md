# Resend

**In one line:** a dark, quiet homepage for an email API, with a big serif headline on the left and a puzzle-like black cube on the right.

## Colour
Pure black (#000000) canvas with faint grey light streaks sweeping diagonally across the lower half and a slightly lighter edge on the right. Text runs from near-white (#FEFEFE) to cool grey (#A1A4A5). The only colour is a thin multicolour gradient border on the small announcement badge. Buttons are dark grey with a visible border; there is no saturated accent in the first screen.

## Type
The headline "Email for developers" is about 96px in a light, high-contrast serif on two left-aligned lines, with the text gradient fading from white at the top to grey toward the bottom of each word. Body copy is 18px grey Inter, and nav and buttons are about 15px sans. Serif display on a developer tool looks surprising and reads as editorial.

## Layout and rhythm
A 1230px container. Nav: wordmark left, seven links centred, Log in and a bordered Get started button right. Hero is two columns at mid-height: left column with badge, headline, paragraph and two actions (a dark bordered Get started button and a plain Documentation text link), right column with the cube about 420px wide. The area below is dark and empty, with light streaks.

## Shape, depth and texture
The cube is a 3D render in the style of a puzzle, each face made of different dark materials: brushed metal, perforated mesh, rough stone and matte black, with highlights on a few edges. The announcement badge is a pill with a gradient outline. Buttons have a 12px radius, a dark fill and a 1px light border. A faint vignette darkens the corners.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
Most developer sites use mono and a code window. Resend uses a luxury serif and a metallic object, which makes email delivery look like a crafted product.

## Steal this
- Apply a vertical white-to-grey gradient fill to large serif display text on black, for a sheen that feels like metal.
- Use a single rendered 3D object with mixed materials as the hero and no other imagery.
- Make the primary button dark with a 1px light border, not a bright fill, so the CTA stays quiet on black.

## Not for
Pages that need to show code or an interface at first glance; the hero carries no product detail.
