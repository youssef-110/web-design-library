# Are.na

**In one line:** a platform for collecting ideas that explains itself as a short document on a white page, with a product window pushed underneath.

## Colour
White canvas, charcoal #333333 text, a pale grey #F7F7F7 for secondary buttons and the Log in tag, and a very dark navy #00075F for the Sign up button. The tokens list Times New Roman as the body font, but the page sets everything in a neo-grotesque sans.

## Type
One sans family at a large reading size, about 24px, regular weight, line height near 30px. The lead line "Are.na is" is followed by a lettered list; bold appears only inside the paragraph to highlight "15 years and 100 days".

## Layout and rhythm
A single 580px column centred on the page, with a miniature star glyph at its top, then the definition list, a paragraph, and two small buttons. A 1px rule full width separates the intro from the next section, titled "How it works", bolded at 18px. Below, a product screenshot in a rounded frame offset left, a phone mockup at the lower left, and a vertical step list (Capture, Arrange, Search, Connect, Network) on the right with a dotted line.

## Shape, depth and texture
Corners are 3px. Buttons are flat blocks. The screenshot frame has a very faint shadow and 24px radius, the only soft element on the page. The photo inside the screenshot (mirrored gold sculpture) is the only image.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
Startups open on a hero image and a verb. Are.na opens with a definition formatted as an interactive list whose last item is a blank, which fits a product about curation and adds a prompt to participate.

## Steal this
- Format your value proposition as a lettered or numbered list with an empty final slot as a call to contribute.
- Keep one large text size for the whole intro and use grey letters for list labels to build structure without headings.
- Show the product in a faint-bordered frame with a vertical step list beside it, highlighting the active step in black and the rest in grey.

## Not for
Brands that rely on strong imagery or emotional impact in the first screen.
