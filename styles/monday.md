# monday.com

**In one line:** a work-management homepage that looks like a consumer app: white, a single violet, round everything, and real people mixed with cartoon agents.

## Colour
White canvas, near-black text (#222222) and one periwinkle violet (#6161FF), used on the Get Started pills in the nav and hero, and on the selected chip tint. The outlined Contact sales button uses a violet border and text. The hero cards bring the only other colours: a person in a coral shirt, teal and yellow 3D avatars with orange glasses.

## Type
Poppins at regular (400) weight, about 56px, left aligned on two lines: "The AI workspace for people and agents". It is not bold; the roundness of the letters carries the friendliness. The paragraph below is about 20px regular in near-black.

## Layout and rhythm
Split hero: text column on the left (about 540px wide), a card cluster on the right. The text order is headline, three-line paragraph, a two-row group of pill chips (Marketing selected, then Operations, IT, Product, Sales, HR, PMO), a violet Get Started pill with an arrow,. Below, a centred line, "Trusted by over 60% of the Fortune 500", over a single row of grayscale logos,.

## Shape, depth and texture
The right side holds three tall rounded cards, overlapping and slightly staggered: a 3D agent called Brief Creator on the left, a real photograph of a smiling man labelled Dan, Marketing Lead in the middle with a typing prompt field overlaid, and a 3D agent called Creative Generator on the right. Buttons and chips are full pills.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
Chips for role selection (Marketing, Operations, IT) sit inside the hero, so the visitor can personalise the message before scrolling. The card cluster mixes a real employee with cartoon agents, showing people and AI side by side, which is the product's claim.

## Steal this
- Add a row of role or use-case pill chips below the hero paragraph, with the first one pre-selected, to let visitors tailor the demo.
- Show a human photo between two stylised agent avatars in overlapping tall cards to illustrate collaboration.
- Use regular-weight rounded type at large size instead of bold, with a single accent colour on all primary buttons.

## Not for
Brands that want a serious enterprise look.
