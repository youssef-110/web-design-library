# Granola

**In one line:** an AI meeting notepad that reads like a book jacket: oversized serif title on white, and a collage of printed textures behind the app.

## Colour
White canvas and charcoal (#292929) text. The only interface colour is an olive green (#5B6F00) on the Download for free pill, with cream text (#FCFCF8). A small lime tag reading New sits in a pill above the headline. The collage on the right brings in chartreuse, black halftone patterns and a red, orange and blue printed flame texture, none of which touch the interface elsewhere.

## Type
The headline "The AI notepad for back-to-back meetings" is a regular-weight transitional serif (Quadrant) at about 110px, left aligned over four lines with tight leading and slightly tight tracking. The sub copy, "Notes, actions and memory. Without a meeting bot.", is about 24px regular grotesque.

## Layout and rhythm
Then a two-part hero. Left column, about 660px wide: New chip, giant headline, sub copy, olive pill button,. Right: a layered collage starting about 1050px from the left edge, running off the right and bottom edges.

## Shape, depth and texture
The collage layers a lime-green sheet, a black photocopy pattern, a red-orange halftone print and big pale lettering with a cream app window (traffic-light dots, a note titled Q3 GTM sync, a date chip and a dark Generate notes pill) and a small video-call frame showing two faces.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
The headline is a serif, much larger than the usual tech sans, and the app is shown against print collage rather than a gradient. The olive button avoids the usual blue and purple of AI tools and gives the page a paper, stationery tone.

## Steal this
- Set a four-line serif headline at roughly 8 times body size, left aligned, and let the app window carry the rest of the visual weight.
- Build the product image as a collage of grainy print textures with the UI window layered on top, instead of a plain gradient.
- Choose a muted olive for the one button with a cream label, so an AI product looks like stationery.

## Not for
Products that want to look futuristic or technical.
