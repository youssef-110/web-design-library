# Framer

**In one line:** a website builder page that shows its editor as the hero, lit by a blue glow on a black ground.

## Colour
Black (#000000) background and white text, with grey for supporting copy. The primary button is white with black text. The only saturated colour is electric blue (#0099FF) in the glowing frame around the hero editor, with panels in the product UI at #1A1A1A. Showcase photos and template thumbnails bring other colours into the lower half.

## Type
A tight grotesque for headlines at roughly 34px (two lines, white, left aligned) with a grey single-line subhead and small 14px interface text. Section titles further down ("The website builder for shipping standout sites", "Shipped with Framer", "Not just vibes, a full platform") are small, left aligned and white.

## Layout and rhythm
Content sits in a column about 800px wide inside a 1280 viewport: logo and nav at the top, a white Sign up pill at right, then headline, subhead, a white "Get started for free" button and a "Connect with" row of icons, with a "New: Image and Shader Generation" link at the far right. The hero panel is a large dark rounded rectangle containing an editor with an agent chat at the right and a blue neon outline around the canvas. Below are client logos in a grid, a second large UI screenshot, three small feature columns, an agent walkthrough that fades in, a mosaic of site screenshots, a logo carousel, a marketplace window and a prompt box.

## Shape, depth and texture
Panels have thin dark borders and small radii; the glow is the only depth effect. Screenshots are shown as complete app windows with title bars.

## Motion
Not observed. A timer in the corner of the hero panel (0:36) shows the demo is a playing video.

## Why it does not look generic
The product is demonstrated, not described: the hero is the editor running an agent task, with the prompt and its progress readable on screen. Blue appears only as light, never as a fill.

## Steal this
- Make the hero a live-looking product panel, including the chat prompt and status lines, instead of a static mockup.
- Use one saturated colour only as an outline glow around the hero object on a black field.
- Keep all page text in a single 800px left-aligned column so wide screenshots break out of it.

## Not for
Products without a screen to show, or audiences who expect warmth. A black page with tiny type is hard going for long reading.
