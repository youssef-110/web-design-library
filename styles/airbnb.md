# Airbnb

**In one line:** a marketplace homepage with no hero image at all: a search bar, then edge-to-edge carousels of home photos.

## Colour
White page and near-black text (#222222). The only strong colour is raspberry on the circular search button, and the coral logo outline (#FF385C).

## Type
Airbnb Cereal, a round open sans, in medium weight. Row titles ("Stay in Ocean City", "Popular homes in Outer Banks") are about 22px, left aligned, with a small round arrow button beside them.

## Layout and rhythm
Very dense. A dark promo bar, then a white sheet with large rounded top corners holding a nav row (3D illustrated tab icons for All, Homes, Hotels, Experiences, Services, then Become a host and two round buttons). Below it, a centred white search pill about 850px wide with three fields (Where, When, Who). Then each row is seven equal photo cards across the full width of the viewport at 24px gutters, with carousel arrows at the right of each title line.

## Shape, depth and texture
Photos have about 16px rounded corners and a heart at the top right and a white pill badge, Guest favorite, at top left.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
There is no hero: the search bar is the hero, and the page begins with real inventory. Every card has the same size and the same badge, so the page reads like a contact sheet rather than a marketing layout. The only decorative element is the set of 3D tab icons, kept to a small size.

## Steal this
- Start a marketplace page with the search control and go straight to equal-size photo rows, with no banner.
- Place small overlays on each card (a white pill badge at top left, a heart at top right) for consistent hierarchy.
- Use a uniform seven-card row with titles and arrow buttons for each category.

## Not for
Products without strong photography for every item. Brands that need a message or value proposition before showing inventory.
