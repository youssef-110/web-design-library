# N26

**In one line:** a European mobile bank that opens on a moody, cinematic photo of a man on a lawn with his phone, so banking looks like leisure.

## Colour
Warm off-white #FAF8F5 for the nav strip and body sections, a deeper beige #EDE7DF for the Log in pill. Primary actions use a very dark green #003B39 and a brighter teal #088177 serves as link and accent. The hero photo is dominated by dark greens and shadow, which the green buttons echo. The nav button and the hero button swap polarity: dark green on cream above, white on photo below.

## Type
The headline is about 80px, medium weight, in a wide, extended geometric sans, centred and white over the image with slight negative tracking. A 20px regular subline sits below at lighter opacity. Nav text is 16px regular. The extended face is the only distinctive typographic choice.

## Layout and rhythm
A slim cream top bar with a 1400px inner width: stacked-N logo and four links left, Log in, Open bank account and a language switcher right. The hero then fills the entire viewport width and almost the whole screen height, with centred text pinned to its top third so the subject and phone occupy the lower two thirds. A small white QR card is fixed in the bottom right corner.

## Shape, depth and texture
Buttons are fully rounded pills. There are no card shadows on the page; depth comes from the photograph, with a heavy dark vignette on the tree canopy, film-like grain and warm light on the subject.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
Fintech sites default to a phone screenshot and a gradient. N26 puts a person in a real, slightly dark location and lets the headline float above the shadows, and the product only shows up as the phone in his hand.

## Steal this
- Place a centred headline in the dark upper area of a photo and keep the subject in the lower two thirds so text never fights the face.
- Set a cream, not white, nav strip over a dark hero so the transition between chrome and image is warm.
- Flip the button colour on dark imagery (white pill) versus on light chrome (dark green pill) to keep one shape and two states.

## Not for
Products that must show feature detail immediately; the first screen contains no UI at all.
