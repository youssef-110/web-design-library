# Ramp

**In one line:** a left-aligned, high-contrast homepage for spend management, where the hero graphic looks like a document being analysed by AI.

## Colour
White canvas, near-black text, and a vivid yellow-green (#E4F222) used on exactly two things above the fold: the See a demo nav button and the Get started for free button. A black strip (#1A1919) holds the announcement and the dark Get started nav button. The hero panel below is light grey with a subtle dot grid. Lime is always paired with black text.

## Type
The headline is about 64px, medium weight, on one left-aligned line ("Time is money. Save both."). The subline is about 19px grey. Tiny uppercase labels (a live counter, "AGENTS AT WORK TODAY") sit in small tracked caps with numbers in boxed chips. The mix of a large friendly headline with data-style micro-labels gives a finance-meets-lab voice.

## Layout and rhythm
A 1310px container from x=305. Left-aligned stack: eyebrow with a boxed live number, headline, subline, then an email field with the lime button docked inside. Under that, a full-width light-grey panel (about 1310px wide) with a dotted background and four floating fragments: a PO and invoice matching card, a Loft Labs invoice, a "Flagged for review" note and an auto-coding form. A ticker bar at the bottom lists running counts of invoices processed.

## Shape, depth and texture
The fragments have corner dots and thin outlines like selection handles in a design tool, and are drawn in tiny type with grey placeholder lines. The page background carries a faint dot grid. Buttons and fields use 8px corners with no shadow. A small boxed counter in monospace-like type adds an instrument feel.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
Instead of a dashboard screenshot, the hero shows the work itself, an invoice with callouts, which tells you what the product does in one glance and gives the page a diagrammatic personality.

## Steal this
- Use one loud accent for just two buttons and pair it with black text, so it reads as a signal and not a theme.
- Draw hero graphics as annotated documents with selection-handle dots and tiny labels instead of generic dashboards.
- Add a live-looking ticker bar of counters at the fold to suggest the product is running right now.

## Not for
Brands that need a quiet, trust-first tone; the acid accent and diagrammatic hero feel experimental.
