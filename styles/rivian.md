# Rivian

**In one line:** an EV maker selling outdoor life, opening on a moving shot of the SUV in golden-hour hills.

## Colour
Photographic and neutral: green hills, a teal-blue vehicle, a pale sky, amber road lines. The interface itself is white and black, with the amber #FFAC00 on the Demo drive button echoing the road paint. The page then drops to a light grey (#F2F2F2) section where a gigantic black model name begins.

## Type
The face is Adventure, a compact grotesque with tight tracking. The slide headline is about 56px, white, left aligned, three short lines on the picture, with very tight word spacing. The next section's black "R1S" model name is at huge scale, cropped by the fold. Nav and buttons are 14 to 16px medium.

## Layout and rhythm
The hero is full width and about 850px tall, with a nav bar floating 12px in from the edges as a rounded white strip: wordmark in widely spaced capitals left, five links centred, amber button plus avatar right. Copy sits at a 120px left margin vertically centred. A circular arrow button on each side, a pill of five slider dots at bottom centre, and an award badge in white on the right fill the rest.

## Shape, depth and texture
Pills for the buttons ("Design yours" white filled, "Book a drive" white outline), slider and arrows, with a 12px radius on the nav. The photo has motion blur on the road and guardrail, giving speed without any overlay graphics.

## Motion
The slider has dots, arrows and a progress bar in the active dot. The photo itself shows a panning-blur capture.

## Why it does not look generic
The truck sits low in the frame and the hills bank sharply across the picture, so the page looks like a magazine cover for a trip. The nav is a card hovering over the photo instead of a bar attached to the page.

## Steal this
- Float the nav as an inset rounded card over the hero so the image bleeds around it.
- Pair a filled white pill and an outlined pill for primary and secondary actions on photography.
- Follow a full-bleed carousel with a pale grey section whose headline is huge and cropped at the fold, to pull the eye down.

## Not for
Enterprise or B2B products with no vivid physical product to photograph.
