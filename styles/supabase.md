# Supabase

**In one line:** a restrained white page for a Postgres platform, where every product gets its own small bordered tile.

## Colour
The canvas is near-white and text is almost black. A single deep green (#0E7E4E) carries the page: the second headline line, the main button and the terminal command highlight. The brighter logo green (#3ECF8E) appears only in the mark. Borders are light grey; nothing else is coloured.

## Type
Manrope at about 46px, regular weight, left-aligned in two lines, the second line coloured green. Beside it sits a short grey paragraph in Inter, about 14px. Card titles are about 16px medium with a 14px grey description in which key words are darkened, giving scannable emphasis without bold.

## Layout and rhythm
A centred container with the heading block split: headline left, description right. Under it a bento grid: one wide card (Postgres, about half the width) beside two narrower ones, then four equal cards below. Each card has a title with icon, a two-line blurb, and an illustration anchored to the bottom or right.

## Shape, depth and texture
Cards are white with a 1px grey border, about 12px radius and no shadow. Illustrations are monochrome line art: an outlined elephant mark, a wireframe globe, an auth list of blurred emails, a wireframe cube with green dots. Buttons are 8px, with a solid green primary and an outlined secondary.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
The product is many services, so the homepage shows each as its own diagram card in the same line-art language, instead of one hero screenshot. Green is rationed so the grid feels engineered.

## Steal this
- Highlight key phrases in a card's grey description by darkening them, not bolding.
- Colour only the second line of a two-line headline with the accent to split promise from outcome.
- Build the features area as a bento of bordered cards of unequal widths, each with a monochrome diagram anchored to a corner.

## Not for
Consumer or emotional products; the diagrams and grey copy are too cool.
