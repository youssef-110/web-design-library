# Family

**In one line:** an Ethereum wallet whose homepage is a stage for cheerful flat-vector characters, with the pitch in the middle and almost nothing else on the page.

## Colour
The interface is white, graphite (#171717) and a pale sand (#F6F4EF). All saturated colour comes from the illustrations: sky blue, vermilion red, emerald green, marigold yellow, with beige discs behind them.

## Type
The headline "Your favorite crypto wallet." is a medium-weight (500) geometric sans at about 64px in two centred lines, dark graphite with tight tracking. Support copy is two short centred lines of regular Inter at around 17px in grey.

## Layout and rhythm
A symmetric three-column hero. The middle column is only about 430px wide and holds the headline, copy and two buttons. Left and right columns each hold a loose cluster of characters and props (a blue flower with a square face, a heart, coins, a wallet, a cat, a bee) that spill beyond the edges of the screen.

## Shape, depth and texture
Buttons are pills: a dark grey Download on iOS with an Apple icon, and a sand Watch the Video with a play icon.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
Crypto brands usually go dark and technical. Family makes the characters the interface, giving an app about assets the feel of a children's sticker book, and keeps the actual UI almost invisible. The wide empty centre gutter makes the headline feel like a caption for the artwork.

## Steal this
- Build the hero as a symmetric three-column: narrow text in the middle, two big illustration clusters at the edges that bleed off-screen.
- Keep the UI neutral (white, graphite, sand) and let swappable illustrations carry all the colour.
- Pair a dark primary pill with a pale sand secondary pill with an icon in each, so the choice reads without outlines.

## Not for
Brands that must signal institutional safety. Sites without an illustrator, since loose character clusters look cheap if drawn poorly.
