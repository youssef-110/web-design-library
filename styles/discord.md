# Discord

**In one line:** a chat platform presented as a game poster, in deep indigo space with a big 3D character scene next to a shouted headline.

## Colour
The background is a rich indigo gradient that gets nearly black at the top and brightens to ultramarine (#1A2081) toward the middle, scattered with tiny stars and blurred floating props. White carries all text. Blurple (#5865F2) fills one button and a lighter indigo (#31368E) is the surface tone.

## Type
The headline GROUP CHAT THAT'S ALL FUN & GAMES is an ultra-heavy all-caps display face, about 56px in three tightly packed lines, with a tight line height so the lines almost touch. Body is white regular at about 20px with a loose measure of about 480px.

## Layout and rhythm
A full-width dark nav with the logo at far left, nine menu items centred and a white Log In pill at right. The hero is two halves: text at left starting about 300px from the edge, vertically around the upper third, and a large 3D tableau at right. Two buttons are centred at the bottom of the hero, well below the text, so the call to action sits in the horizontal middle of the screen regardless of the split above.

## Shape, depth and texture
The illustration shows a tilted desktop monitor with the chat app, a phone with video tiles, a game controller, a small robot and two characters, all rendered as soft glossy 3D with blue rim lighting. Buttons are 16px rounded rectangles: white with a download icon, and blurple.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
The brand's own mascots, controller and chat window are staged together as a game box art scene rather than a screenshot. The ultra-heavy all-caps type and the star field make the page look like a launch trailer, which suits a gaming audience.

## Steal this
- Place a staged 3D scene of the product plus its mascots on a dark gradient with scattered blurred props for depth.
- Set the headline in an ultra-heavy all-caps face with tight leading, left aligned, with a plain regular paragraph beneath.
- Put the primary and secondary buttons side by side in the exact centre below the hero, apart from the left-aligned text.

## Not for
Reading-heavy or professional products.
