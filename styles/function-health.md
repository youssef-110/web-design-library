# Function Health

**In one line:** a lab-testing service that opens like a luxury film: a golden-hour profile portrait on a dark hillside, glass diagonals and one soft italic line.

## Colour
The hero is a dark, warm photograph: brown and gold hillside grass, a deep amber skin tone and a blue-grey sky behind. Over it, text and the Start testing pills are cream (#FEF9EF). The top announcement bar and the button text are a burnt terracotta (#B05A36).

## Type
The headline "Check your health." is an italic serif at regular weight, about 72px, left aligned in cream, with an HSA/FSA Eligible pill tag above it. Stats at the bottom right use a medium-weight sans at 22px with a lighter 15px caption: 160+ lab tests, Whole body, $1 per day, separated by thin vertical rules.

## Layout and rhythm
A terracotta announcement bar, then a rounded translucent nav panel about 1440px wide with the white logo (an orb of curved lines and the word Function in light weight), five links, Log in, a cream Start testing pill, a search icon and a menu icon. The photo fills the rest. All copy sits in the bottom 40 percent: tag, headline, paragraph and button at left, a three-stat row at right on a shared baseline.

## Shape, depth and texture
The portrait is cut by diagonal translucent bands that repeat and refract the face, like panes of glass, running from upper left to lower right.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
The photograph is a person in nature, not a lab, and the diagonal glass effect adds a sense of scanning or layers without using a medical icon. The italic serif gives a literary, intimate voice to a testing product.

## Steal this
- Overlay a full-bleed portrait with repeated diagonal translucent bands to suggest layers or analysis without a graphic.
- Anchor all copy in the lower third and put a three-stat row at the right on a shared baseline, separated by thin vertical rules.
- Use an italic serif headline with a cream colour over a dark photo and a button with accent-coloured text on a cream pill.

## Not for
Pages that need to show prices, tables or comparisons at once.
