# Hungry Tiger

**In one line:** an Indian sauce brand that sells one jar through huge saffron type, colour blocks and tilted product photography.

## Colour
The hero is rust brown (#883814) with a faint dark leaf pattern and visible grain, and the headline and buttons are saffron (#FAAE33). Later sections swap the whole background: saffron (#EB9F36), near-black chocolate (#281006) and hot pink (#CC245B) with a pink floral print behind the jar photos. Each block is a single flat colour, never a gradient.

## Type
A heavy, wide geometric sans in capitals. The first headline "BOLD FLAVOR" spans almost the full viewport width and takes roughly a fifth of the screen height; the line under it ("TIKKA MASALA") is about half that size. Small body copy and labels are set in a condensed caps style. Several later section titles are low-contrast tone-on-tone and appear to fade in as you scroll.

## Layout and rhythm
Top: pill-shaped nav buttons at left (Sauce, About, Recipes, Game), the logo centred, Contact and Buy Now with a basket at right, with a dotted yellow rule under them. The hero has a second dotted rule between the two headline lines and the rotated jar overlapping both. A small caption and a Buy Now pill sit bottom left. Sections then alternate: long empty brown stretches with a lone title, a dark-and-pink split panel (ingredients left, hands holding the jar on pink right), a saffron three-column "why the jar matters" with square photos, tilted polaroid-like photos scattered on saffron, a recipe row of three cards, and a newsletter split with a huge "HUNGRY TIGER" footer.

## Shape, depth and texture
Nav and buttons are fully rounded pills. Photo cards are square-cornered, some tilted a few degrees. Texture comes from the grain and leaf print on brown. No shadows.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
The brand colour is used as a ground, not an accent: the whole screen is spice-coloured and the jar is the only object. Pink print backgrounds behind the product photos are borrowed from Indian textile colour, not from a food-brand template.

## Steal this
- Set the first headline to span the full viewport width and tuck the product across it.
- Change the entire background colour at each section so scroll position feels like turning a page.
- Tilt supporting photos a few degrees on a flat colour field instead of aligning them to a grid.

## Not for
Brands needing a calm, trustworthy tone, or pages with dense information. The long empty scroll stretches and loud colour would frustrate users.
