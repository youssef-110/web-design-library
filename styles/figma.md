# Figma

**In one line:** a product page that stays black and white and lets sample designs carry all the colour.

## Colour
The interface is only white (#FFFFFF) and black (#000000), with grey for secondary lines. Buttons are solid black with white text, and the secondary "Contact sales" and "Log in" are outlined. Colour appears only inside sample artwork: a violet poster, a green landscape, turquoise and yellow illustrations, a red poster in the footer area, plus the small five-colour logo in the header.

## Type
One neutral grotesque with tight tracking, regular weight. The hero headline "The full-stack creative canvas" is set in three short lines at roughly 40px at 1280 width, left aligned. Section headings are small and quiet: a black sentence followed by a grey continuation in the same paragraph ("Move fast in the right direction on an AI-native canvas." then grey support text).

## Layout and rhythm
A thin header with logo, four dropdown links, and Log in, Contact sales, Get started at right, then a hairline. The hero is three columns across the width: headline left, a stacked group of overlapping design cards in the centre, a rounded black "Get started" button at the right. A round pause button sits under the cards. Below, large areas of empty white separate sections, each starting with a short left-aligned sentence. Product examples appear in a staggered grid of square tiles with play icons, then a testimonial with a big "86%" figure and a logo strip, a full-width black "Get started" bar, a community template row and a black footer.

## Shape, depth and texture
Buttons are rounded rectangles (about 8 to 16px), cards in the stack are square with slight overlap and no shadow. The footer is solid black.

## Motion
Not observed. The pause button and play icons show the media animates.

## Why it does not look generic
The tool's own interface is not used as decoration. Instead the hero stage shows finished design work, stacked like cards on a table, and everything around it is black text on white.

## Steal this
- Keep every interface element black or white and let user-made work provide all the colour.
- Stack three or four overlapping sample outputs in the middle of the hero rather than one screenshot.
- Write section headings as a black sentence followed by a grey continuation in the same block.

## Not for
Brands with no vivid work product to show; the neutral frame needs colourful content.
