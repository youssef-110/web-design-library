# Wispr Flow

**In one line:** a voice dictation app presented as a printed page, with an old-style serif headline and lines of spoken text drifting around it.

## Colour
Pale butter yellow (#FFFFEB) canvas and near-black (#1A1A1A) text. A thick deep teal-green announcement bar tops the page and the same green fills a small status pill ("Removed repetition"). The one accent is a pastel lavender (#F0D7FF), used for both Download for free buttons, each with a dark outline and dark text.

## Type
The headline "Don't type, just speak." is in EB Garamond at regular weight, around 90px, centred over two lines, with the second line in italic. The paragraph is a 22px regular Figtree. The serif against the geometric sans makes the page feel like a book with a modern caption.

## Layout and rhythm
Teal bar, then a boxed floating nav panel centred at about 900px wide containing a logo, a pill toggle, three links and a lavender button with a dark border. The hero is centred with a very large gap above it, about 190px, then eyebrow, headline, paragraph, one button, small availability line. Below, a green status pill sits above a white waveform capsule that merges into a black ribbon of text crossing to the right edge.

## Shape, depth and texture
Transcribed sentences in grey run in a looping circle at the left and a long arc along the bottom, as if the voice drew them.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
The product is voice, so the hero is made of words on curved paths and a waveform, not a screenshot. The serif with an italic second line makes dictation feel like writing rather than software, which is unusual in AI tooling.

## Steal this
- Illustrate a voice or text product by running its real output as grey text along curved paths around the headline.
- Set the second line of a serif headline in italic to add rhythm without changing size.
- Use a pastel button fill with a thin dark outline instead of a saturated colour, so the accent feels printed.

## Not for
Interfaces that need strong status colours and dense controls.
