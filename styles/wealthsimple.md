# Wealthsimple

**In one line:** a Canadian investing brand that opens on a dark, glossy campaign scene: a golden coin on deep navy, with a bold giveaway headline.

## Colour
The first screen is a dark navy gradient fading to near black at the edges. The only warm colour is the coin, a metallic gold with copper shadows. White carries the headline, the Enter now pill and the floating nav panel (#FCFCFC). Black pills (#1C1B1B) appear on the white panel.

## Type
The headline "We're giving away $1 million (again)" is medium-weight (500) white sans at about 64px, left aligned on two lines. The brand wordmark in the nav is a bold serif, while navigation labels are 15px medium sans.

## Layout and rhythm
A banner and a floating white nav panel (about 1400px wide, 12px radius) sit at the top, inset from the screen edges, with the wordmark, seven menu items with chevrons, a search circle, an outlined Log in and a black Get started pill. The hero is then split: text and form at left, vertically centred at about a third of the screen height, with the 3D coin at right, about 520px wide and rotated, running toward the edge.

## Shape, depth and texture
Imagery is a photoreal 3D render of a coin with a serif W, softly lit, with ridged edges, on a dark vignette. Form elements are pills: an outlined email field with white label and a solid white Enter now button.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
Investing sites normally show phone screenshots. This opens with a single glossy object that stands for the prize and the brand, lit like jewellery, and lets a serif wordmark and tiny serif eyebrow carry the premium tone.

## Steal this
- Hero object: render one hero item in 3D with a brand letter on it, and place it off-centre on a dark vignette.
- Float the nav as an inset white panel with rounded corners on a dark hero, so it reads like a card.
- Add a small serif eyebrow above a sans headline to give an editorial tone to a product page.

## Not for
Brands without a prize or object to render.
