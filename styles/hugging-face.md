# Hugging Face

**In one line:** an open-source AI hub that uses its homepage as a live catalogue, with a dark browsing panel up top and white lists of what is trending.

## Colour
White page, near-black navy hero (#0B0F19), and the emoji's yellow (#FFD21E), which appears in the logo, the heading emoji and small accents. Text is navy-black (#101828) on white and white or light grey on the panel. Spaces cards bring gradient colour in blue and orange, one per card.

## Type
Source Sans in semibold at about 60px, left aligned over two lines in white on the panel; body is 18px light grey. The page chrome is small, 14px. Model names are set in a monospace face, such as moonshotai/Kimi-K2.5, so they read as identifiers.

## Layout and rhythm
A full-width nav with a search field, five product links with tiny icons and a black pill Sign Up. The hero panel is a wide rounded rectangle whose upper edge steps up at the right, where a tilted model-browser fragment (task chips, parameter slider, library pills, a list of models with download counts) runs off the panel. Below, "Trending on this week" in a centred line, then three columns for Models, Spaces and Datasets, each a card list.

## Shape, depth and texture
Dark panel with a curved-bottom edge, pill and small-radius chips, hairline borders on light cards. The hero's inset content is real UI at small scale with a soft perspective that fades toward the right.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
The hero shows the platform's actual filter vocabulary, tasks, libraries and providers, as if the site were itself a model. The hugging-face emoji mascot keeps an infrastructure company approachable.

## Steal this
- Fill a dark hero with a cropped, perspective-tilted view of your own interface, running off the edge.
- Set machine identifiers in monospace within otherwise humanist sans copy.
- Use a one-line "Trending on [mascot] this week" divider to introduce live content columns.

## Not for
Brands with little data to show; the hero depends on a rich catalogue.
