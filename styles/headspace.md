# Headspace

**In one line:** a meditation app homepage that feels like a children's book: soft sunrise gradient, a smiling orange circle and large friendly type.

## Colour
White canvas, with a hero band that fades from peach at the top to pink at the bottom. The brand orange (#FF7E1D) is the mascot disc and the logo dot. The call to action is electric blue (#0061EF), the opposite of orange, so the eye lands on it instantly.

## Type
One friendly grotesque, Apercu, at medium to semibold (500 to 600). The hero headline is about 64px on two lines, left aligned and dark brown.

## Layout and rhythm
The left half holds headline, one line of support, a blue pill and legal copy; the right half holds the mascot. Below, back on white, a centred heading introduces a three by two grid of equal outlined cards.

## Shape, depth and texture
The mascot is a large orange circle with a smile line, wearing a tilted blue price tag that says 50% OFF, with small clouds and sparkles around it. Cards below are white with a thin grey border (#D2D5DE), 8px radius, a label on the left and a small colourful icon plus chevron on the right.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
The mascot is an actual character that carries the offer, not a stock photo, and the gradient suggests dawn. Using blue for the single button against an orange and pink hero gives a maximum colour contrast that most wellness sites avoid in favour of pastel harmony.

## Steal this
- Put your one action in the complement of your brand colour (blue against orange) so it cannot be missed.
- Turn a promotion into a prop held by your mascot instead of a banner.
- Follow the hero with a grid of identical outlined cards, each with a small icon at the right, as a gentle self-selection step.

## Not for
Clinical or serious mental health contexts where playfulness could feel dismissive.
