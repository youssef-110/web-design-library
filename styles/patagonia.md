# Patagonia

**In one line:** an outdoor clothing store that leads with a real mountain photo of real people, not a product shot.

## Colour
The chrome is white and black: a black strip at the top for Activism, Stories and Find a Store, white around the logo, search and nav. The tokens list a plum, a navy and a bronze link colour, but they stay out of sight on this screen. Colour comes entirely from the photograph (snow, a red shell, a pale green jacket) and from tiles below it.

## Type
Ridgeway Sans, a friendly humanist-geometric sans. The headline is about 64px, medium weight, tight leading, left aligned in white at the lower left of the photo. An all-caps 16px eyebrow sits above, a 18px regular paragraph below. Main navigation is 18px regular, black on white, separated by plain spacing, no dividers.

## Layout and rhythm
Full-width stacking: black utility bar (48px), nav row with logo left and a grey pill search plus three icons right, category links in a second row, then a hero photo about 710px tall and full bleed. Text starts at an 80px left margin, far from the subjects on the right, which keeps the faces clear. Under the hero, a horizontal row of four-and-a-half photographic tiles with a cut-off edge suggests scrolling.

## Shape, depth and texture
Four white pill buttons (Women's, Men's, Kids' and Baby, Packs and Gear) sit in a row under the paragraph. Tiles have gently rounded corners. No shadows; the grain of the photo is left natural and slightly cold.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
Shops lead with a model in a clean studio. Patagonia shows two skiers mid-trek in a dull blue light and sets a campaign line on it, so the brand's stance (use gear hard) is visible before any product is.

## Steal this
- Offer several category pills directly in the hero so the campaign image doubles as navigation.
- Align hero copy to a left margin and keep the focal subjects on the opposite half of the photo.
- Use a thin black utility strip for secondary links to keep the main nav clean.

## Not for
Brands whose products need studio-clean presentation; an expedition photo hides small product details.
