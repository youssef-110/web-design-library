# Amie

**In one line:** an AI meeting-notes app's homepage that reads like a long product article inside a narrow column.

## Colour
Off-white page (#FAFAFA), black headings, grey supporting text (#5C5C5C), and one bright blue (#11A8FF) on the primary buttons. A soft peach-pink (#FBEBE9) and tiny coloured labels (green, blue, purple eyebrows over sections) are the only other colours; product screenshots add more.

## Type
Inter throughout. The hero headline "Run your workday on autopilot with AI agents" is bold with tight tracking, about 38px at 1280 width, over two lines and left aligned. A grey two-line subhead sits under it. Section headings further down are smaller bold lines, each with a coloured eyebrow label above and some phrases highlighted with a pale grey or blue marker block ("Within 47 seconds:", "in just 7 days").

## Layout and rhythm
A column roughly 650px wide, centred in the 1280 viewport, holds everything. The header has a small black logo, Features and Pricing at left, Login and a blue Get started pill at right. The hero stacks: a Product Hunt pill and an App Store laurel badge, the headline, the subhead, a blue "Get started" button next to a white "Download" button, a small changelog line, and a large bordered screenshot of the app that runs off the first screen. Below, one section after another: logo strip, a big statement, long feature sections with ratings, screenshots, integration diagrams, FAQ rows and a footer link grid.

## Shape, depth and texture
Primary buttons are 12px blue blocks; the secondary white button has a hairline ring and tiny soft shadows. Screenshots have thin borders and small corner radii. Backgrounds are flat, and the page is long and text-dense.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
The page tells the product's value in running sentences and proof lines instead of icon cards, and uses highlighter marks on phrases. The hero shows the real notes document at near-full size rather than a stylised mockup.

## Steal this
- Put a social-proof badge row above the headline, then the headline, then a screenshot that bleeds off the bottom of the first screen.
- Mark the key phrase in a sentence with a pale highlighter block instead of colour or bold.
- Add a small coloured eyebrow label above each section title to code topics by colour.

## Not for
Visual brands or products that depend on an immediate emotional hook; a narrow text column with long copy tires casual visitors.
