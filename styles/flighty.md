# Flighty

**In one line:** the marketing page for an iOS flight tracker, which uses plain heavy type and a staged cascade of real notifications to show what the app tells you.

## Colour
White canvas with black headline and grey support copy. The accent is a warm signal yellow (#F7BE00), used for the active tab in a dark bottom bar. A narrow top announcement strip is a deep indigo gradient.

## Type
The headline "Get the truth when you travel" is set very heavy (about 800 weight) at roughly 65px on a single centred line with tight spacing, in black. It uses the platform system font, so on Apple devices it matches the app itself.

## Layout and rhythm
Strict centre axis. Announcement strip, then a nav with logo, links and a Get the app link, then the headline, paragraph and badge row, with the phone starting about 450px down and running off the fold. The phone is held by a photographed hand. On both sides, rows of white notification cards (Mom landed in New York, Check In is now open, Gate changed, Flight changed) stagger in height and fade to nothing toward the outer edges. A dark rounded tab bar floats at the bottom with Preflight, At the airport, After landing and Download.

## Shape, depth and texture
The phone shows a satellite-style map with flight arcs and a My Flights list. The tab bar is a black pill with a yellow active segment.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
The page explains the product with its actual notifications, written like real messages, instead of feature bullets, and arranges them like a feed around the device. The headline is deliberately plain and heavy so all the delight sits in the cards.

## Steal this
- Fan real product notifications or messages on both sides of the device and fade the outer ones to transparent.
- Add a floating dark pill tab bar at the bottom of the hero to switch between moments in the user's journey.
- Use the platform system font at heavy weight for the headline so the page matches the native app.

## Not for
Products without a clear stream of short messages to show.
