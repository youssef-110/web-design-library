# Perplexity

**In one line:** a plain, cream-white app screen that opens straight onto a question box, built for people who just want to ask.

## Colour
A very pale cream canvas (#FCFCF9) with a slightly greyer sidebar (#F3F3F3). Text is a dark teal-ink (#0B2833) with slate (#475D65) for secondary labels and icons, and hairline borders (#D7DBDD). The only dark spots are a black Got it button on the cookie panel and the black circular send button inside the input. Small coloured blobs appear only in a "Hire your first teammate" card.

## Type
The prompt "What do you want to know?" is about 28px in a regular-weight sans, centred and dark teal. Sidebar items are about 15px regular, descriptions about 13px grey. There are no bold headlines anywhere on the screen; hierarchy comes from size and ink strength alone.

## Layout and rhythm
A 240px sidebar on the left (New, Computer, Automations, Artifacts, Plugins, a collapsible Teammates card, Projects, Recents, a View plans chip and a Sign In row). The rest of the screen is blank except for a centred column of about 640px: the logo mark, the question, a large rounded input with a plus icon, Search and Computer toggle chips, a model selector and a mic button, then two side-by-side suggestion cards. The vertical centre of the viewport sits at the question; the top third is empty.

## Shape, depth and texture
The input is a tall rounded rectangle with a thin border and no shadow; the toggle is a small pill pair with one filled white. Suggestion cards use a faint grey fill with an icon, title and two-line description. The logo is a geometric asterisk-like mark made from thin angular strokes. A cookie notice floats bottom-right as a bordered card.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
There is no hero, no pitch and no screenshot; the entry point to the product is the page, so the design is entirely about the input box and its hierarchy of options.

## Steal this
- If your product starts with one input, make that the homepage: one prompt line, one large box, two suggestion cards.
- Replace black text with a dark brand-tinted ink (#0B2833) and a slate secondary, on a cream canvas.
- Use a slightly greyer fill for a permanent sidebar and keep the main area one tone lighter, with no borders between them.

## Not for
Products that need persuasion before sign-up; this screen assumes the visitor already wants to use it.
