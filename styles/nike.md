# Nike

**In one line:** a sports retailer homepage built as a stack of full-width campaign posters, each followed by a short row of products.

## Colour
White page, #111111 text, pale grey tiles (#F5F5F5) behind product shots. The one strong flat colour is a burnt orange promo banner (#D73902) at the top. Campaign sections bring in their own colours through photography (icy blue, olive green, orange-red) and nothing else is tinted.

## Type
A very heavy, tight condensed face in capitals for headlines ("GREATNESS WRAPPED UP", "LOCK IN TO LIFT OFF", "THE FUTURE OF AIR. BY YOU"), set in two or three stacked lines with minimal leading. Under each headline sits a small grotesque caption and one or two small buttons. Navigation and product captions are small Helvetica-style text.

## Layout and rhythm
Top: a thin utility bar, a white nav with a search pill, then the orange promo banner with dashed white dividers. The hero is a full-bleed photograph of four people in winter clothing, with the headline anchored bottom left. After it come three square image cards, then a repeating module: a small centred brand mark (Jordan, All Conditions Gear, Run, NSW, Skims), a full-width photograph with bottom-left headline, and a row of three product tiles on grey. Some modules split the width into two halves. A single "Trending" block shows tiny product icons in two rows.

## Shape, depth and texture
Media is square-cornered; interface controls are pills (search field, buttons). No shadows. Photography is high-contrast, stylised and often tinted to one colour.

## Motion
Not observed. Arrow buttons beside each product row show they scroll sideways.

## Why it does not look generic
Each sub-brand introduces itself with its own logo mark above its poster, so the page feels like a magazine of campaigns rather than a product grid. The headline position never changes, which makes the shouting readable.

## Steal this
- Fix the headline to the bottom left of every full-bleed image and repeat that spot in every module.
- Introduce each sub-brand with a small centred logo above its image block.
- Follow every poster with exactly three product tiles on pale grey so the poster sells and the tiles convert.

## Not for
Products without a stream of dramatic photography, or services that need dense text. The poster rhythm collapses without images.
