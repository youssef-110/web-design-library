# Revolut

**In one line:** a banking brand presented as a lifestyle campaign: sky, a face, a thin white outline and one very large, light headline.

## Colour
The first screen is a photo of a clear blue sky with a model in a linen shirt; the interface adds only white and black. Headline, nav and body are white; the primary button is a black pill; the nav Sign up is a white pill.

## Type
Aeonik Pro at regular (400) weight, about 136px for "Banking & Beyond", set on one line from the left margin, in white. Supporting copy is about 24px white regular on a short measure, left aligned under the headline.

## Layout and rhythm
Standard wide container. The headline starts about 150px below, spanning most of the width. The body copy and black Download the app button sit at lower left; the model photographed from the chest up stands centre, overlapping the second half of the headline so the letters pass behind her head.

## Shape, depth and texture
A tall rounded outline (white 2px stroke, around 40px radius) frames the model like a phone screen, with the account label, balance and a white Accounts pill centred inside it and a pale card edge peeking at the bottom.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
The model overlaps the headline, so type and person sit in the same space. The phone is suggested by a bare outline rather than a mockup, and the interface adds no colour, so the brand feels like a fashion campaign. Light-weight huge type does the opposite of what bold fintech headlines do.

## Steal this
- Make the hero photo full-bleed and let a large light-weight headline pass behind the subject for depth.
- Draw a device as a thin outline frame holding real data instead of a full mockup.
- Keep interface colour to black and white pills so any photo can be swapped in without clashing.

## Not for
Dense information pages. Brands that need a consistent recognisable colour independent of the current photo.
