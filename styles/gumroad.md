# Gumroad

**In one line:** a platform for creators to sell things, introduced with a bouncy, sticker-like toy economy on a clean cream page.

## Colour
Cream (#F4F4F0) canvas, black text and buttons, one hot pink (#FF90E8) used for the coins and the logo badge. Illustrations use a small supporting set of teal, sunny yellow and pink fills with thick black outlines. A violet #625BF6 sits in the stylesheet but not in this view. Pink never fills a button, so the actions stay black and solid.

## Type
ABC Favorit in regular weight throughout. The headline is about 96px, centred, one line, with open tracking and the numerals 0 and $1 as the focus, in a quirky grotesque with a single-storey g. Sub copy is 22px, nav and buttons 18px. The wordmark is a rounded, heavy custom lowercase face that is the only bold type on the screen.

## Layout and rhythm
Full-width white nav strip with a 1px bottom rule: wordmark and a GitHub star pill left, links centred-right (the current one, About, in a black pill), and Log in plus a solid black Start selling block bleeding to the right edge. Below, a vertically centred hero with a black button and a search field fused side by side, and six coins scattered at the corners, each bleeding off the viewport edge. Cards below begin around y of 780, overlapping an illustration that breaks out of its frame.

## Shape, depth and texture
Coins are 3D discs with a thick black stroke, offset side thickness and a pink face; they are tilted differently to feel mid-toss. Illustrated product windows use heavy black outlines and a hard offset shadow, a neo-brutalist sticker style. Search and buttons are square, cards are generously rounded.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
Marketplaces default to product grids. Gumroad uses a single idea (earn the first dollar), draws money as flying coins, and gives the hero a toy-like personality while keeping controls austere.

## Steal this
- Scatter 3D objects at the corners of a centred hero, each cropped by the edge and tilted differently, to add energy without crowding the text.
- Fuse a primary button and a search field into one row of identical height.
- Combine thick black outlines with a flat hard shadow on illustrated UI windows.

## Not for
Finance or legal tools where playfulness reduces perceived reliability.
