# Nothing

**In one line:** a consumer electronics brand whose site looks like a technical manual from a friendly lab: grey, monospaced and see-through.

## Colour
A cool light grey canvas (#F4F4F4) with near-black text. The only chroma on this first view is a flat yellow #FFC700 on the main action in a region popup, plus the pink of one earbud case in the photo behind it. A location modal in pale grey (#DFDFDF) covers the middle of the screen; everything else described here is visible around and behind it. Products are shown in greyscale with transparent shells.

## Type
Three voices. A serif-like typewriter face (NType82) for the large headline at about 48px, in the lower left over the photo, with an airy, slightly irregular character. Small uppercase monospaced labels for buttons and captions ("SHOP NOW", "BLACK FRIDAY") at about 12px. A pixel dot-matrix face in the brand's logo and in occasional display lines. The tokens list 20px body text, but labels are noticeably smaller.

## Layout and rhythm
Edge-to-edge product photography with copy anchored at the bottom left corner, a 32px margin and a rectangular Shop Now button. Products are cropped by the viewport, tilted and overlapping, so the frame feels dense with objects rather than text. The popup is a tall centred panel with a stack of centred mono lines and a yellow rectangular button above an underlined text link.

## Shape, depth and texture
Everything is rectangular with zero radius. The products carry the texture: clear polycarbonate, visible components, rubbery coloured silicone, soft studio shadow.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
Gadget sites lean on glossy gradients. Nothing builds a grid of grey, label-like type and see-through parts that make the interior visible, so the aesthetic argument (transparent, honest, a bit nerdy) is made by the typography itself.

## Steal this
- Use uppercase monospaced microlabels for buttons and captions to give a product site a lab-tag character.
- Show the product in near greyscale and keep one flat saturated colour for the single main action.
- Crop overlapping product photos off the viewport edges and anchor copy to a bottom left corner.

## Not for
Brands selling comfort or care, where a technical mono look reads as cold.
