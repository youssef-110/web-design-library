# Raycast

**In one line:** a dark, high-energy homepage for a keyboard launcher, built from a single dramatic red-striped hero and one big download button.

## Colour
Near-black canvas (#07080A) with white text. The hero is filled by a field of diagonal slabs in rose-red (#D63953) with blurred, grainy edges and thin blue-navy shadows between them, running from top-left to bottom-right across the viewport. The brand red (#FF6363) appears in the small logo. Buttons are light grey (#E5E5E5), the only light objects on the page.

## Type
The headline "Your shortcut to everything." is about 64px in a semibold Inter, white, centred on two lines over the stripes. The subline is about 18px white. Under the main button, small monospace lines (platform requirement, Homebrew install, Download V1) in grey give a developer-terminal flavour. White type over saturated red is readable because the slabs are mid-tone and the text is heavy.

## Layout and rhythm
A floating nav bar with a rounded outline holds the logo, nine links, Log in and a light Download button. The hero is centred on one axis in a faintly outlined frame about 1200px wide: headline and subline at the centre of the stripes, then a large gap, then a single Download for Mac button with an Apple icon and two mono lines beneath. There is no product screenshot in the first screen.

## Shape, depth and texture
The nav and hero frame have about 20px corners and a thin low-contrast border. Buttons are 8px-radius light keys with a soft glow and a subtle top highlight and bottom edge, like physical keys. The red slabs carry film-grain noise that gives them a printed look and softens the colour.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
The page offers no interface mockup; the red grainy slabs suggest speed and light, and the single big key-like download button turns the CTA into the product's own object.

## Steal this
- Fill a hero with one large abstract shape field (diagonal slabs) and add film grain so it looks tangible, with white heavy type on top.
- Build the primary button like a keycap: light fill, top highlight, bottom dark edge, faint outer glow.
- Put small monospace install lines under the CTA to add developer credibility without extra sections.

## Not for
Products that need to show what they do immediately; this hero is mood only.
