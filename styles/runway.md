# Runway

**In one line:** an AI video company that opens with one cinematic frame in a white page, as if the website were a gallery wall.

## Colour
The page is white with charcoal text (#262626) and a mostly neutral palette. Colour comes from two places only: the blue-to-violet announcement bar above the nav, and the hero film still, which is warm orange and ash grey. Partner logos are grey and the nav buttons are light grey (#EEF1F5) and charcoal.

## Type
A neutral grotesk (ABC Normal) in regular weight, about 56px for the headline, left aligned in white on the image over two lines. The supporting sentence is about 15px white. Lower on the page, the next headline repeats at the same size in black, centred. Nothing is bold or tracked.

## Layout and rhythm
Announcement bar, nav (wordmark left, seven menus centre, three buttons right) and then a wide rounded frame about 1140px by 490px holding the hero video. The headline, sentence and Try Runway for free button sit in the lower left corner of the frame. A logo strip follows, then a large centred headline introducing three platforms.

## Shape, depth and texture
Corners are modest: 6px buttons and a gentle radius on the hero. The hero is a dramatic photographic frame with strong light and a vignette, so text is placed over the dark area. There are no shadows or borders.

## Motion
Not observed. The hero is a film frame and is a video.

## Why it does not look generic
The product is moving imagery, so the page gives its whole first screen to one film frame and uses almost nothing else. The white buttons on the picture make interface and content feel like one surface.

## Steal this
- Make the hero a single rounded media frame with headline, sentence and one button anchored to its lower left.
- Pair a deep image with a plain white page and a neutral grotesk so the image carries all the emotion.
- Use a coloured announcement strip as the only colour chrome above the nav.

## Not for
Products without strong footage or imagery; the hero depends entirely on the frame.
