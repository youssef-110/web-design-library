# Stripe

**In one line:** a bright, confident homepage for financial infrastructure, built around one huge gradient ribbon and a long, two-tone headline.

## Colour
White canvas, with a dark navy announcement bar (#151F3F) on top. The headline is split into two tones: the first clause in deep navy (#061B31), the rest in a lighter steel blue (#40668D), so the opening claim reads first. The violet #533AFD fills the Get started and Contact sales buttons. The ribbon artwork brings in orange, pink, violet and pale blue; it is the only multi-colour element.

## Type
Sohne-Var at about 48px in a light weight, left-aligned across four lines, with relaxed leading. Light weight at large size makes a long headline readable as a paragraph. A small eyebrow above reads "Global GDP running on Stripe" followed by a live-changing percentage. Nav and body are about 14px regular.

## Layout and rhythm
Content sits in a roughly 1260px column, bounded by two full-height 1px vertical rules, which reveal the underlying grid. The headline starts at the column's inner margin and runs most of the width, with two rectangular buttons below it. A logo strip (Cursor, OpenAI, Amazon, Nvidia, Ford, Coinbase) sits between horizontal rules at the fold, and the next section's headline repeats the two-tone treatment.

## Shape, depth and texture
Buttons are rectangular with 4px corners: a solid violet one and a white one with a hairline border carrying a Google icon. The ribbon enters from the top right, runs diagonally and passes behind the headline text, softly blurred at its edges with a fibrous, brushed texture. There are no shadows.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
A ribbon of coloured light is a distinctive hero object, and it is overlapped by the text instead of being kept to a side panel, so type and image share the same space.

## Steal this
- Split a long headline into two tones (dark first clause, mid-blue remainder) so one sentence has a built-in hierarchy.
- Let a hero graphic run behind the text and bleed off the edge instead of boxing it in a column.
- Draw the layout grid with 1px full-height column rules and logo strips between horizontal rules, so structure is visible without cards.

## Not for
Brands that need a calm, text-first page; the ribbon and long light headline compete for attention.
