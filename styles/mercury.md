# Mercury

**In one line:** a dreamy, photographic homepage for online business banking, where the hero is a misty valley with a lone desk and laptop.

## Colour
The first screen is a full-bleed photograph in dusk tones: pink-peach sky, blue-grey haze, deep forest greens. Text is off-white (#EDEDF3). The single saturated colour is indigo (#5266EB) on the Open account buttons, which pops against the muted image. A near-black #171721 appears in the legal disclaimer bar at the bottom. The nav floats transparently over the photo.

## Type
The headline is about 62px in a light, regular-weight humanist sans, centred on one line, with a 22px subtitle in the same face below. The wordmark is small letter-spaced capitals with a circular, interlaced emblem. Large light type over a soft photo reads as calm and confident rather than technical.

## Layout and rhythm
Everything is centred on one axis: nav (logo left, five links centred, Log in and an indigo Open account pill right), then headline, subtitle, and a docked email field. The field is a pill with placeholder text and an indigo Open account button inside it, flanked by a ghost Launch demo pill. The lower two thirds of the viewport is just the photograph, with the desk placed at the lower centre as the focal point and a dark disclaimer bar pinned at the very bottom.

## Shape, depth and texture
All controls are pills. The image is graded with a heavy vignette and soft depth of field, with a hand-painted look. The desk, chair and laptop are crisp against the soft landscape, which makes them read as the subject. There are no cards or shadows in the hero.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
Finance sites show cards, charts and phones. Mercury shows an empty desk in a landscape, an image about leaving the office behind, which is an emotional claim a bank rarely makes.

## Steal this
- Use a single full-viewport hero image with a central subject and leave the top third for centred type; the image's vignette keeps text legible.
- Dock a signup input and its button in one pill, with the button inside the field's right end.
- Keep the accent to one saturated colour (indigo) on the CTA only, and let a muted graded image supply everything else.

## Not for
Pages that must show product screens or compliance detail above the fold; the photograph carries no information about the service.
