# Pitch

**In one line:** a presentation tool that opens with a purple stage, a huge headline and a prompt box, then moves through white sections and a deep indigo finale.

## Colour
The hero is a violet gradient (#5318EA lightening to lavender at the top left) under a lavender announcement bar. Lower sections go white with indigo (#280F62) cards, and the page ends in near-black indigo. One lime (#C4EE87) button, Get a demo, sits beside a white pill; a coral outline ring marks the prompt box. The tokens read light, but the hero and the closing band are dark violet, so the theme is mixed.

## Type
A bold, round geometric sans (Mark Pro) at about 180px in white, set tight over two lines, with a slight tonal fade on the first letter. Section headings are about 48px dark on white. Body copy is 16px regular; the template names behind the hero use varied faces, serif and condensed, to show range.

## Layout and rhythm
Hero: tiny logo top left, hamburger top right, headline centred, then a rounded frame with the prompt (with highlighted tokens for Pitch, HubSpot and Granola) and floating app icons, then one line and two buttons. Faded template slides scatter across the background at several sizes. Below, centred sections of cards in bento layouts and a long testimonial strip of bright cards.

## Shape, depth and texture
Large rounded panels, pill buttons, soft inner glows. The blur-and-fade of the background slides creates depth without shadows.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
The background is the product's own output, a collage of real decks in many styles, and the prompt field sits in front as if you were about to make the next one.

## Steal this
- Fill the hero background with dimmed, scattered product outputs at different scales instead of a gradient alone.
- Highlight key tokens in a sample prompt with tinted chips to show inputs from connected apps.
- Use one off-system lime button against a purple palette to mark the sales action.

## Not for
Serious, conservative B2B brands; the volume of colour and type size is loud.
