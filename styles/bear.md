# Bear

**In one line:** a friendly, airy page for a Markdown notes app, built on white space and one realistic screenshot.

## Colour
The page is pure white with mid grey text (#444444). The red-pink (#DD4C4F) belongs to the bear logo, links and accents inside the screenshot such as footnote numbers and the selected note marker. Purple (#884AA8) is a secondary accent. The only dark area is the screenshot's charcoal sidebar.

## Type
A custom rounded-geometric sans (Bear Sans) at about 22px for body and about 51px for headlines lower on the page. Weights are light and regular; the nav is small grey regular. The roundness echoes the bear mark.

## Layout and rhythm
A very generous top margin, then a compact nav row: logo left, six links right on a single baseline, in a column about 650px wide. Centred below: a device tab row (Mac, iPhone, iPad) with a hand-drawn squiggle and little sparks over the active one, then an App Store badge, then a large screenshot with a drop shadow. Sections after that alternate centred headline and screenshot.

## Shape, depth and texture
The screenshot has a rounded window with a soft shadow, showing a note list and an article with a photo, a table and inline code tags. The squiggle underline and sparks are the only hand-drawn flourish.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
The hand-drawn underline, rounded type and a polar bear photo in the sample note give the app a personality of its own while the page stays minimal. Choosing platform with tabs puts the download first.

## Steal this
- Draw a small hand-made squiggle under an active tab to add warmth to otherwise geometric UI.
- Use realistic sample content, like a note with a photo, instead of filler text in the product screenshot.
- Put a platform switcher above the download badge so the screenshot below changes with it.

## Not for
Brands that need density or authority; the white space is generous to the point of sparse.
