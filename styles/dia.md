# Dia

**In one line:** an AI browser's homepage that looks like a literary magazine, in saffron yellow and cream.

## Colour
The first screen is a blurred saffron-yellow field (#FFCB30) with soft darker blotches. Below it the page turns to warm off-white (#FBFAF6) with white cards and thin grey borders. Blue-violet (#5863EA) and mustard (#E1B746) are the scraped link and accent colours, while the product windows bring cream, peach and pastel tints.

## Type
Display text is a high-contrast serif. The tagline "More than a browser. A better workday." is white serif about 36px; the "DIA" wordmark is a colossal swash serif, roughly 400px tall, cropped by the window below. Section titles ("Dia reads between the tabs", "Built for how you actually work", "Privacy first") are centred dark serif. Card text is small sans. Vertical dates and times in the demo ("15 SEP 2026", "08:45 AM") run up the sides of the mock newspaper in a condensed serif.

## Layout and rhythm
A small white floating nav pill sits top centre (logo, What's New, Apps, Security). Then the tagline, the giant letters and a Mac window overlapping them. Underneath, a curved cream edge starts the second part: centred headlines, a black "Join the Waitlist" pill, large product screenshots, then a two-column grid of six bordered feature cards, each with a small caps label, a sentence and an illustration. A privacy block with toggle chips and a closing headline end the page.

## Shape, depth and texture
Cards have thin borders and rounded corners; the nav is a pill. Screenshots show the app as a real window with traffic lights, a cream sidebar and an editorial "Tuesday Brief" with a floral painting.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
Software pages seldom use ornamental serif letters this large. The product is demonstrated by a generated newspaper page, so the app's output looks like print, and the yellow is soft and out of focus.

## Steal this
- Use a giant wordmark in an expressive serif as the hero and let a product window cover its lower edge.
- Show the product's output as a styled editorial document rather than a UI screenshot.
- Float a small white nav pill over the hero instead of a full-width bar.

## Not for
Utilitarian or enterprise tools where decorative type reads as unserious, and pages needing fast scanning of specs.
