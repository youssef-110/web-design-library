# Aesop

**In one line:** a hushed skincare retailer that opens on a blue-grey, softly filmed close-up of its own packaging, with white type floating over it.

## Colour
The hero is a cool, desaturated blue-grey photo with a dark gradient at the bottom for legibility; all text over it is white. Below the fold the canvas turns a warm cream (#FFFEF2) and the top announcement bar is charcoal (#333333). The red-orange tones #CA432F and #FF816B exist in the tokens as accents, but nothing on this first screen uses them. Restraint is the point.

## Type
The wordmark top centre is white and set in a refined serif. The hero headline is a light serif, about 30px, centred; the eyebrow above and the paragraph below are small regular sans (14 to 16px). Navigation is 14px sans in white, widely spaced across the centre. Weight changes are minimal; size and case make the hierarchy.

## Layout and rhythm
Strictly centred. A 57px announcement bar, then a hero about 960px tall with utility links (Stores, Customer service) top left, account links top right, logo centred and a single row of ten categories plus a boxed search field underneath. The slide copy sits low centre with a rectangular outlined button. Play and mute buttons bottom left and three thin progress bars bottom centre.

## Shape, depth and texture
Everything is square and outlined: the search field and the Discover button are 1px white rectangles with transparent fill. There are no shadows. Texture is photographic, a slightly blurred, film-like video still.

## Motion
The hero is a video with play and mute controls and a three-slide progress indicator, so it advances automatically.

## Why it does not look generic
A shop would normally open on a product grid. Aesop opens on a mood image of its own packaging and hides the shop under one quiet menu row, so the site feels like a boutique, not a catalogue.

## Steal this
- Make the hero video full bleed and put the nav over it in white, with a dark bottom gradient only where the copy sits.
- Use outlined rectangular buttons with no fill on photo backgrounds to avoid colour clashes.
- Centre everything and let one light serif line carry the emotional beat.

## Not for
Stores with large catalogues where shoppers expect immediate product browsing.
