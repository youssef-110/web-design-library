# Obsidian

**In one line:** a quiet black page for a private notes app that shows the app itself, graph and all, immediately.

## Colour
Near-black (#0F0F0F) with a faint paper-like noise, light grey text (#EEEEEE) and one violet. The solid violet (#7C3AED) fills the download button; the lighter lavender (#A88BFA) colours the logo gem, links, tags and checkboxes inside the screenshots. Graph nodes are grey and white with a few green points.

## Type
A bold system sans at about 60px for the headline, tightly set over two lines and left aligned, followed by a lighter grey subhead at about 36px, which is a deliberate step down in weight and tone. Navigation and UI text are small, 13 to 14px.

## Layout and rhythm
A single centred container with left-aligned content, wide margin on the right left empty. The order is nav, headline, subhead, a large violet download button with a text link for other platforms, then a wide app screenshot. The screenshot's right edge fades out while a phone mockup sits low on the right, overlapping the window.

## Shape, depth and texture
Buttons are 8px, the app window has traffic-light controls and a thin border, and the phone has a tight rounded bezel. Depth comes from overlap of the phone on the window, not shadow. The graph view is the one decorative image, a constellation of dots and lines.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
The hero screenshot is rich in content (a real note, a tag, a quote, a graph) so the product's idea is visible without a diagram. The download button names the visitor's detected platform.

## Steal this
- Make the primary button name the visitor's platform ("Get Obsidian for Linux") and put "More platforms" as a plain link beside it.
- Overlap a phone mockup across the corner of a desktop screenshot to show cross-device use in one image.
- Drop the subhead weight and brightness one step from the headline so two sentences read as a pair.

## Not for
Brands needing colour or personality in the hero; the black page and screenshot depend on a strong product UI.
