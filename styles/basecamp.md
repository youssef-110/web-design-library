# Basecamp

**In one line:** a project management tool that sounds like a person: plain statements, underlined links, a hand-drawn arrow and giant screenshots.

## Colour
White frame, a very pale blue panel (#F5F9FF) behind the hero, and charcoal-slate text (#29353C) rather than black. The only saturated UI fills are the blue Sign up button (#006AD2) and the blue underlined links (#146DC7). The logo mountain in green and blue is the only other colour apart from the screenshots, whose photographs (a city dusk, wood-and-sky gradient, warm brown) add tone.

## Type
Graphik at about 66px, bold and very tightly tracked so letters nearly touch, centred in two lines. The sub line is 28px regular in a lighter grey. Everything else is 17 to 20px with default underlines on the links, which are set in a row like a sentence. The handwritten caption ("This is what Basecamp projects look like") sits in a slanted script with curved arrows.

## Layout and rhythm
A centred symmetrical page: a sign-in tab left, the logo in the middle, the Sign up button right, all on white strip. Under it the pale panel contains a link row, a small video-tour card, the headline and the sub head. Then three huge screenshots appear side by side, the middle one fully visible and the two flanking ones cropped by the page edge, with the page gutter at 40px.

## Shape, depth and texture
Everything is square-cornered, with only faint shadows around the screenshot cards and the tour card. The screenshots use rich photography as backgrounds behind the white UI to show how real teams use the tool.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
SaaS pages hide long text under icons. Basecamp prints actual links as underlined sentences, shows a live count of people using it, and draws on the page with a pen, so the site reads like a founder's letter.

## Steal this
- Set links as an underlined row of plain-language phrases, including a live statistic, instead of an icon nav.
- Add one hand-lettered caption with curved arrows to point at the product shot and humanise the page.
- Show screenshots at oversized scale and let the flanking ones be cropped by the viewport to imply abundance.

## Not for
Consumer luxury or fashion, where the plain underlined style would look unrefined.
