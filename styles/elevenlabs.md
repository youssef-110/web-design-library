# ElevenLabs

**In one line:** a pale, quiet homepage for a voice and speech platform, where the only colour comes from soft, grainy spheres.

## Colour
Almost everything is neutral: a warm off-white canvas, a slightly darker #F5F3F1 stage, white raised pills and black text and buttons. Colour is held back for the orbs in the demo carousel (orange, violet, brown, mint), and a thin peach gradient on the announcement strip at the very top. The black pill is the only strong accent.

## Type
The headline is about 52px in a regular-weight grotesque, set on two left-aligned lines with tight leading. The supporting paragraph sits far to the right at roughly 17px in Inter, and carousel captions are small and grey. Black text on the large headline against grey captions gives a clear three-level hierarchy without any bold weights.

## Layout and rhythm
Content sits in a roughly 1180px column. The hero is split: headline and two buttons on the left, a short paragraph floating top-right on the same band. Below is one large rounded stage with a three-tab switcher (product names with small dot icons), a horizontal carousel of orbs with the active one centred and enlarged, left and right arrows, and a row of pill chips plus a black Sign up at the bottom. The upper half of the screen is generous white space; the stage fills the rest.

## Shape, depth and texture
The stage panel has about 16px corners with a hairline border, and everything interactive is a pill. Each orb is a grain-textured gradient sphere with a play button on the centre one, which gives a tactile, painted feel against flat UI. Depth comes only from tone steps between canvas, stage and white pills.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
Voice AI usually means waveforms and dark neon. Here the sound is represented by soft, grainy spheres, each a different mood, which turns "voices" into objects you want to pick.

## Steal this
- Show variety with a carousel of abstract textured objects (grain over a gradient) instead of stock imagery, enlarging the active one to about 1.3 times its neighbours.
- Put the headline left and a short descriptive paragraph far right on the same band; the empty middle makes the hero feel designed.
- Contain the demo in one rounded, slightly darker stage with a tab switcher on top, so product lines share a single focal area.

## Not for
Products that must show real data or screens above the fold; the abstract orbs say nothing about function.
