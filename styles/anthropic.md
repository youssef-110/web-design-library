# Anthropic

**In one line:** a warm, text-led homepage for an AI company, opening with a mission statement and a vast, soft photograph of the planet's edge.

## Colour
A parchment canvas (#FAF9F5) with near-black text (#141413). The nav is made of small pills with a slightly darker warm fill (#F5F4ED) and a hairline border (#E8E6DC). There is no brand colour in the UI; colour comes from the photograph: a peach-orange sky above a blue water-like curve. That image sits in a rectangle that fills almost the full width.

## Type
The headline is a paragraph of about 48px that mixes two voices: the first sentence in a bold sans, the following two sentences in a light serif, all in one flowing block of three lines, left-aligned with about 80px margins. The mix signals "claim, then explanation", and the large serif body makes the page read like an essay.

## Layout and rhythm
Navigation is split to the edges: pills for News, Research, Policy, Commitments on the left, the spaced capital wordmark centred, and Company and Try Claude pills with search and menu icons on the right. Under the nav sits the statement, then a wide hero image beginning at about y=360 that fills the content width (about 1780px) and runs well below the fold. There are no buttons in the first screen.

## Shape, depth and texture
Controls are small pill chips with chevrons. The image is a blurred, grainy photograph (a curved horizon of deep blue ocean against a warm sky) without frame decoration or overlay text. Corner radius on the image is minimal. Nothing has a shadow.

## Motion
The wordmark is built as a Lottie animation in the markup; other motion not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
There is no call-to-action button, no product screenshot, and no gradient hero. The page opens with a mission paragraph and a photograph of the planet, which positions the company as institutional and unhurried.

## Steal this
- Set the page's lead statement as a large paragraph that switches from bold sans to light serif mid-block, rather than a short slogan.
- Make the nav out of small, hairline-bordered pills split to the screen edges, with the wordmark alone in the centre.
- Fill the hero with a single huge photograph that has no text over it, and let it run past the fold.

## Not for
Pages that must drive a click in the first screen; there is no button.
