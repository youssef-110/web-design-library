# Lovable

**In one line:** an app builder that frames a prompt field in a glowing gradient arch, with a tight bold headline in the middle.

## Colour
The page is cream (#FCFBF8) with near-black type and black pill buttons. All saturated colour sits in one hero gradient: a pale blue haze that deepens to #5185FE, then magenta pink (#FD84E5) and red (#FE364B) at the bottom edge. The gradient is a mirrored arch that leaves a light centre where the prompt box sits. A small coloured logo cube in the nav echoes it.

## Type
Camera Plain in bold at about 48px for the headline "Build something Lovable", tight tracked, centred on a single line, with an 18px grey subtitle below. Nav is 14px regular. The heavy weight against the soft gradient keeps the line legible.

## Layout and rhythm
A slim nav with logo left, six links centred, a Log in pill and a black Get started pill right, on a 860px wide row. Directly under it, about 250px of pure cream gradient fade, then the centred headline, subtitle and a white rounded prompt box (plus button, Build dropdown, microphone). The gradient hero ends in a hard straight edge at the bottom and the cream page resumes.

## Shape, depth and texture
Soft and round: pill buttons, a prompt card with a large radius and gentle shadow, and a blurred gradient with no noise. The sharp horizontal cut at the end of the gradient makes the hero read like a stage.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
The hero has no screenshot at all. The prompt box is the product, and the gradient sunrise supplies the emotion in the brand name, "Lovable".

## Steal this
- Mirror a large blurred gradient on both sides of the centre column so the main content sits in a bright clearing.
- End a gradient hero with a straight horizontal edge instead of fading it, to make a stage.
- Make the primary input a white card with plus, mode and microphone controls inside it.

Products that need proof screenshots above the fold; the hero shows none.
