# Replit

**In one line:** an app builder whose homepage is a single question and a prompt field, with very little else competing for attention.

## Colour
Warm cream (#FAF6F1) fills the page and text is a soft charcoal (#37383C), never pure black. Orange (#FF3C00) appears in the logo mark, the Sign up button and the thin ring around the prompt field; a faint peach glow spreads behind the field. Chips and buttons are pale grey-beige with hairline borders.

## Type
Replit Diatype, a neo-grotesk, at about 64px in a regular weight with noticeably tight tracking, centred on one line: "What will you build?". Subline is small, about 14px, grey. Everything else is small sans at 13 to 15px, so the headline is the only large thing.

## Layout and rhythm
A slim nav, then a long band of empty space before the centred headline, so the fold is mostly air. The stack under the headline is: subline, wide prompt box (placeholder cycling through prompts), a row of rounded-square type tiles (Website, Mobile, Design, Slides, Animation) with arrows, then three example-prompt chips and a small circular scroll arrow at the bottom.

## Shape, depth and texture
Soft radii throughout: 8px buttons, a roomy prompt box with an inset send button, icon tiles with light borders. No shadows apart from the glow around the prompt box. No imagery above the fold.

## Motion
A caret blinks in the prompt box and an example-prompt refresh control sits under it.

## Why it does not look generic
The homepage is the product's input field. Nothing explains the tool before you can use it, and the glow marks the field as the one live thing on the page.

## Steal this
- Make the hero a single input with category tiles and example chips beneath, so the first click is the product.
- Pair a warm cream canvas with charcoal text and one saturated orange to avoid cold white.
- Use tight tracking on a single-line display headline and keep the rest of the type small.

## Not for
Products with several audiences or complex pricing, where an empty hero hides what is on offer.
