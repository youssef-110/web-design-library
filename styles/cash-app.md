# Cash App

**In one line:** a payments app that sells itself with one giant product screenshot in a green frame and a lot of untouched white space.

## Colour
White canvas, black type, one neon green (#00E013). The green appears in three places only: the logo tile, the phone bezel and the Get started pill.

## Type
Cash Sans, a tight neo-grotesque, is used at regular (400) weight with compressed line spacing. The headline "The way money should work" is about 56px on two lines, left aligned, with letters set close. Nothing is bold.

## Layout and rhythm
Three-part horizontal composition at vertical centre: headline at far left, the phone dead centre, a short paragraph plus a green pill at right, with roughly 600px of empty white between them. The nav is a bare strip: green logo tile at left, outlined Sign up, black Log in and a black round menu button at right.

## Shape, depth and texture
The phone has a thick green frame with a soft white glow shadow around it.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
The headline is regular weight, not bold, and the type is small relative to the screen, which is unusual for a loud fintech brand. The product is shown at full phone height as the hero, with the bezel painted in the brand colour so the frame itself becomes the logo.

## Steal this
- Colour the device frame in the brand colour, so a plain white page gets a single bold object.
- Split text to the far left and right of a centred hero image and leave the middle gutter empty.
- Use regular-weight type at large size for a headline instead of bold, and let the contrast come from scale and whitespace.

## Not for
Pages that need to explain multiple features above the fold. Responsive layouts where the side-by-side text cannot fit beside the phone on narrow screens.
