# Notion

**In one line:** a white, centred homepage with a huge bold headline and a playful mix of clean product UI and hand-drawn black ink figures.

## Colour
The canvas is white and the text near-black. One blue family carries all colour: #0075DE for the primary button and nav CTA, a pale #E6F3FE for the secondary button and the pill around the headline word "Think", and a brighter blue dot inside that pill. The product mockup brings small pastel colours (tinted kanban columns, status chips), which is where colour variety lives.

## Type
The headline is about 96px, bold and tightly tracked, centred on two lines, with the highlighted word in a rounded pill. A 20px regular subtitle follows, then two 8px-radius buttons. Using the plainest sans at poster scale lets the highlighted pill act as the only decoration.

## Layout and rhythm
Header: logo left, nav centred, Log in and a blue Get Notion free button right. The hero is centred on a single axis: headline, subtitle, two buttons, then a large product window starting about 530px from the top and cropped by the fold. A strip of customer logos (OpenAI, A24, Figma, FedEx, ramp, Cursor, Substack, Nvidia, Toyota) is pinned at the bottom with dot separators.

## Shape, depth and texture
The mockup is a browser-style window with pale traffic-light dots, a sidebar of agents and teamspaces, and a kanban board in soft pastel columns. Black-and-white line illustrations in a sketchy style (a seated figure at lower left, a drooping face at top right) overlap the window's edges, and small coloured emoji-like badges float over the cards. Cards have thin borders and almost no shadow.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
Most workspace sites use a clean screenshot alone. Notion breaks the screenshot's frame with ink drawings that stand outside it, which gives a human, handmade tone to enterprise software.

## Steal this
- Highlight one word in a headline with a pale tinted rounded pill and a small solid dot, as a lightweight "typed word" device.
- Draw monochrome line illustrations that overlap the edge of a product screenshot, so the screenshot feels part of a scene.
- Use one hue at two intensities for primary and secondary buttons, with the pale tint doing the secondary work.

## Not for
Brands that need to look austere or institutional; the sketched characters and bold type are deliberately warm.
