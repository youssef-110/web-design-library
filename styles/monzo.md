# Monzo

**In one line:** a UK app bank that opens on a candid, saturated arcade photo of two people hugging, with the pitch written across the picture.

## Colour
The page frame is white, and the coral (#FF4F40) lives almost only in the lowercase wordmark top left. Interface buttons are deep navy (#091723): the Sign up pill in the nav and, further down, the section headings. On the photo, text and the call to action are white and off-white.

## Type
The headline, "Spend your money on life, not your life on money", is bold (700) in a rounded geometric sans at about 48px, two lines, left aligned, in white over the image. The sub line is a light, regular-weight 20px version in off-white.

## Layout and rhythm
A wide container with 240px side margins. The nav is a small row: coral wordmark, a segmented Personal / Business toggle in a pale pill, and a navy Sign up pill on the right. Directly under it, one large rounded photo panel about 750px tall holds everything: headline and button anchored low left, trust badges (an award roundel, a switch guarantee, a protection lock) low right.

## Shape, depth and texture
The hero photo has very round corners, around 40px, echoing the pills. The call to action is a light grey-white pill with dark text, sitting on the picture.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
Banks tend to open on a phone mockup. Monzo opens on an unposed moment of joy and writes the line on top of it, so the first impression is human, and the product only appears after the fold. The coral wordmark is the one constant brand colour, kept small so the photo does the work.

## Steal this
- Make the hero a single rounded image panel with the headline and button inside it, and keep the nav outside the panel on the white page.
- Keep your brand colour to the wordmark and use a dark navy for every button, so the logo stays distinctive.
- Put trust badges in the photo's corner so credibility sits in the same frame as the pitch.

## Not for
Brands without access to strong, warm photography.
