# OpenAI

**In one line:** a stark white homepage that starts with the product itself, a prompt field, then shifts into editorial cards with dark space imagery.

## Colour
Pure white and pure black, with a mid grey (#666666) for secondary text and light grey (#E0E0E0) hairlines. The only colour sits inside the imagery below the fold: warm gold sparks and a glowing planet on a near-black navy background. Buttons are black pills with white text or light grey pills with black text.

## Type
OpenAI Sans, a clean geometric-humanist grotesk, medium weight at about 24px for the question "What can I help with?". Navigation is 14px regular. Headlines on the story cards are huge, white, heavy sans set across the full card width (the cropped words read "Astra" and "Sol" with a smaller version number between them).

## Layout and rhythm
A narrow nav row, then roughly 200px of nothing before the centred question and a 560px prompt box with an up-arrow send button and five outlined pills (Talk with ChatGPT, Research, API Platform, Stories, More). Another large gap, then a two-column editorial grid: a wide landscape card on the left and a tall square card on the right with a small caption below it.

## Shape, depth and texture
All controls are pills; the prompt box has a thin border and a very soft shadow. Imagery uses dark, grainy, glowing space photography with oversized white type, which contrasts with the bare white page above it.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
It treats empty space as the main design element and puts the product's input at the centre rather than a hero image. The contrast between the white shell and the dark story cards divides utility from storytelling.

## Steal this
- Open with the product's input field, centred in whitespace, with outlined pill shortcuts under it as the navigation.
- Use pill shapes for every control so the whole page has one repeated curve.
- Put dramatic dark imagery in cards below a bare white section so colour arrives only with content.

## Not for
Products that need to explain themselves before use; the opening is silent.
