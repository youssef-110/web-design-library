# Warp

**In one line:** a terminal company page that presents itself as a scientific instrument, with monospace type, figure captions and a live status grid.

## Colour
White and near-black plum ink (#1A1522) with a faint lavender wash that deepens down the page. Electric blue (#2A1EFF) is the data colour inside the figure: only the active squares. A single lemon-yellow (#EEF17C) button, Book a demo, is the only warm note. Light blue-grey (#CDDFEE) is a minor surface tone.

## Type
Azeret Mono at about 54px for the headline, left aligned over two lines, in the ink colour; lowercase mono also sets the buttons and the figure captions. The paragraph below is a regular sans (Matter) at about 16px in grey, so the mono feels like a deliberate voice, not the whole page.

## Layout and rhythm
A left-aligned column about 800px wide, with faint vertical hairlines running the full page height at column positions and horizontal rules crossing them at section boundaries. The headline, copy and two buttons sit at the top left; below, a wide bordered frame with a bracketed caption centred on top contains the dot-matrix figure. A trusted-by logo strip sits between rules under it.

## Shape, depth and texture
Corners are square or 4px, buttons are flat with one-pixel borders, and there are no shadows. The figure is a grid of 8 rows by 28 columns of tiny hatched squares, a few filled blue, with a legend (queued, working, waiting, shipped) and counters in the footer line.

## Motion
Not observed. The figure carries a live label and task counters.

## Why it does not look generic
The hero is a captioned diagram, "fig. 1", instead of a terminal screenshot. The page reads like a technical paper with one product-shaped data visualisation.

## Steal this
- Caption hero visuals in bracketed lowercase mono, such as "[ fig. 1 ]", to turn a screenshot into an exhibit.
- Draw the grid with faint full-height column rules and cross rules, so structure is visible without cards.
- Use one off-palette yellow for the single sales button and blue only for live data.

## Not for
Consumer or lifestyle brands; the mono type and paper-diagram stance are cold.
