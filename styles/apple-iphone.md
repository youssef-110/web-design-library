# Apple iPhone

**In one line:** a product-family index page that behaves like a catalogue: one big title, a row of model icons, then carousels of cards.

## Colour
White canvas (#FFFFFF) with near-black text (#1D1D1F). The cards use a pale grey (#F5F5F7) and one card in the carousel is solid black. Blue is the only interface colour: #0071E3 fills the "Learn more" pill, and #0066CC colours the text links and the outlined "Buy" pill. Everything else is the colour of the phones.

## Type
SF Pro in two cuts. The page title "iPhone" is about 50px at 1280 width, semibold with tight tracking, left aligned. Card titles ("iPhone Duo", "iPhone 18 Pro") are the same weight at a slightly smaller size, with a lighter one-line tagline under them. Section titles ("Explore the lineup.") end in full stops.

## Layout and rhythm
A thin black global nav, a pale banner line, then a 1000px-wide left-aligned column. Under the title is a row of ten small icons with labels (iPhone Duo, iPhone 18 Pro, Compare, Accessories, iOS 27) with "New" in orange under two of them. The first big card spans the column and the next card peeks in from the right edge, so the row reads as a carousel. Below, "Explore the lineup", "Why Apple is the best place to shop iPhone" and "iPhone essentials" repeat the same pattern: left-aligned title, then a row of cards. The page ends in a very long block of small grey legal text.

## Shape, depth and texture
Cards have generous rounded corners, no borders and no shadows. Buttons are pills: outlined blue and filled blue. Imagery is clean device renders and a pair of hands holding the phone, on flat backgrounds.

## Motion
Not observed. Pause and dot controls under the first card show it slides.

## Why it does not look generic
It is a lineup page, not a hero page. Product icons act as the navigation, and each card repeats one structure, so the user compares models by scanning the same positions.

## Steal this
- Put a row of small product thumbnails with labels directly under the page title as the primary navigation.
- Build every card with the name top left and the two actions top right, so the eye always finds them in the same place.
- Let the next card peek in from the right edge to signal a carousel without arrows.

## Not for
Pages that must persuade with long copy or a single flagship message. The catalogue pattern buries a story.
