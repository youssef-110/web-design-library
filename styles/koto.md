# Koto

**In one line:** a creative agency homepage staged as a black screening room: black field, yellow signature, and a statement in tall thin letters.

## Colour
The capture shows an entirely black viewport (#000000), with the media area not yet loaded. Text is white for the first line of the statement and mid grey (#B4B4B4) for the second. The yellow (#FFE800) appears only in the logo at the top left, where a smile curve sits under the word, and the rest of the page is monochrome. Buttons are dark grey (#1F1F1F) with grey text.

## Type
The statement "We're Koto / The Creative Company" is set in a light condensed grotesque at about 53px, in two lines, left aligned at a 30px margin with the second line dimmed. Navigation (WORK, ABOUT, SERVICES, LATEST, CAREERS, CONTACT) and the clock are 12px uppercase mono with slight tracking, at 40px spacing.

## Layout and rhythm
Edge-to-edge with a 32px margin. A single header row: logo, six links, then a "15:25 UTC-5" clock and a grid-dots glyph at the far right. The whole middle of the screen is empty, the statement anchors the lower left corner 30px above the fold edge, and a circular pause button sits at the lower right corner.

## Shape, depth and texture
No shapes, borders or shadows. The logo is a custom lettered mark where the final o's are two rings and a curved underline makes a face. Pause is a thin outlined circle.

## Motion
The pause button implies a looping background video; the clock indicates a live element.

## Why it does not look generic
Studio sites commonly use a bold sans on a white page. Koto uses a black stage with light-weight type, a live clock and a mono nav, so it presents like a control room, and the yellow is rationed to one logo.

## Steal this
- Show a live local time in the header in mono type to make a static page feel inhabited.
- Pair a thin condensed headline in two tones (white then grey) at the lowest left of the screen with tiny uppercase mono navigation.
- Ration a single saturated colour to the logo and let everything else stay black, white and grey.

## Not for
Brands relying on product detail, trust or conversion above the fold; the first screen holds no content beyond the statement.
