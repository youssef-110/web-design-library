# Cal.com

**In one line:** a scheduling product whose headline contains pictures and UI chips, set in a chunky rounded sans on plain white.

## Colour
White canvas, charcoal (#242424) text and buttons, hairline grey borders. Colour is almost entirely absent; the only warm touches are the small orange Booked label and the coloured Google and Microsoft logos on the sign-up buttons. The product mockup is grayscale blur and photography.

## Type
Cal Sans at about 80px, heavy, tightly tracked, centred over two lines. The inline chips (photo of people, a clock tile, an Intro call card) are the same height as the x-height of the words, so they read as extra words. The subtitle is grey Inter at 18px; the nav is small and regular.

## Layout and rhythm
A rounded nav pill spans the container, then a NEW pill, the headline, subtitle, two equal sign-up buttons (dark Google, outlined Microsoft), a small grey line about email sign-up, and an enormous browser mockup. The mockup is a macOS window frame with a Safari window inside showing the booking page: profile at left, a calendar in the middle, time slots at right.

## Shape, depth and texture
Large radii on buttons and nav, soft shadows on the Safari frame. The backdrop is a blurred grayscale photograph so the white UI inside stands out.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
The headline swaps words for working mini-UI (clock, booking chip), so the page demonstrates scheduling before you read the copy. The fake macOS menu bar even says "Design review in 5m".

## Steal this
- Put small live widgets inline in the headline in place of nouns, sized to the type.
- Show the product inside a believable OS and browser frame with a blurred photo behind it.
- Offer two identical-size SSO buttons, one filled and one outlined, as the sole call to action.

## Not for
Brands that need colour or warmth as their identity; this is a monochrome system.
