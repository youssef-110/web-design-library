# Things

**In one line:** a hushed, centred product page for a to-do app, where the app icon is the logo and the screenshot is the show.

## Colour
The canvas is a cool pale blue-grey (#F2F5F7) and text is a soft charcoal (#303336). The only strong colour is blue (#2576EB), used in the app icon, the lighter link "Watch Introduction Video" (#5C9CF5) and the playful list icons inside the screenshot: yellow star, pink calendar, teal stack.

## Type
Plain system sans. "Things" is bold at about 36px, centred under the icon; the description is regular at about 18px in mid grey, four lines in a narrow centred measure. Nothing is tracked or styled; the typography stays out of the way of the app.

## Layout and rhythm
A narrow centred column about 650px wide with a minimal nav (logo left, three small grey links right) over a hairline. Then, stacked and centred: large icon, name, paragraph, a video link, and a long gap. The app window enters from the bottom, rotated and perspective-skewed, cropped off the right edge.

## Shape, depth and texture
The icon is a glossy tray with a checkmark with a soft drop shadow. The app window is a white sheet with a grey sidebar, three hollow traffic dots and a long soft shadow. Corners are generous but quiet.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
The icon and tilted screenshot do all the branding; there is no hero headline, slogan or gradient. The tilted UI shows the real text at an angle, like a card tossed on a desk.

## Steal this
- Lead with the app icon and name, then one paragraph, and skip the slogan.
- Skew and rotate the product screenshot in perspective, bleeding off the bottom right, for energy without motion.
- Keep a single blue and let the product's own colourful icons carry all the other colour.

## Not for
Products that must explain several audiences or tiers above the fold; there is no room for it.
