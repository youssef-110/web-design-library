# Stripe Press

**In one line:** the publisher's shop is a book-as-object stage: one tilted 3D hardcover, one oil painting, and a scrolling ribbon of titles.

## Colour
A pale blue-grey canvas (#DDE6EC) almost the same value as the sky of the painting, so the page and the artwork read as one. Text is warm near-black (#1C1B19). The pink #FF5FA8 and yellow #F4E400 in the stylesheet are absent here; colour comes from the painting (lavender mountains, pink-orange moorland, green firs) and the olive-and-gold cover of the featured book.

## Type
Two voices. The titles are in a tall, tightly set display serif, about 58px, alternating roman for the active book (Nomad Letters) and a slanted italic for the others. The system voice is a small uppercase monospace at 14px for the brand name, "[ CART 0 ]", and "[ FOOTER ]", using square brackets like a command line.

## Layout and rhythm
A thin 1px rule under a mono header, then a one-line title marquee that fades at both edges, then a 1888px by 890px rounded panel that fills the rest of the viewport with 16px margins. The book sits left of centre, tilted around 40 degrees as if falling or flying through the painting.

## Shape, depth and texture
The painting has visible craquelure and a slight glitch of colour-fringed pixels on the moor. The book is a rendered object with a cloth spine, a gilded edge on the page block and a visible gutter, casting no drop shadow, so it feels like it is hovering.

## Motion
The title marquee is cropped at the left and right edges mid-word, so it scrolls; the active title is in roman and the others in italic.

## Why it does not look generic
Book shops use a cover grid. Stripe Press shows one book at a time as a physical thing in a landscape and alternates roman and italic to indicate selection, which gives the store the feel of a gallery wall.

## Steal this
- Mark the active item in a list by switching a serif from italic to roman, with no colour or underline.
- Match the page background to the dominant sky colour of the hero artwork so the panel seems to emerge from the page.
- Use square-bracketed uppercase mono labels for system controls (cart, footer) to contrast with an expressive serif.

## Not for
Catalogues with many items that need quick comparison; this layout shows one product at a time.
