# Rauno Freiberg

**In one line:** a designer's personal site laid out as a horizontal strip of cards on a grey desk.

## Colour
The page is light grey (#EDEDED). Cards are white (#FFFFFF), text is near-black (#171717), and the one colour is a pure yellow (#FFFF00) circle. A second card shows a giant black letter "D". Nothing else on the page is tinted.

## Type
A tight grotesque, regular weight, set large: the intro on the first card is about 50px at 1280 width, six short lines with alternating lines indented (a hanging indent), so the text zig-zags. Labels above other cards ("Devouring Details") are about 10px grey.

## Layout and rhythm
At the top centre is a scrubber made of thin vertical tick marks with one highlighted rectangle, like a film strip or timeline. Below it a row of 800px by 480px cards runs sideways: the first card (white, with the intro and yellow circle) is centred, and the second card starts at the right edge with a huge black "D" cropped by the edge. The remainder of the page below is empty grey; the whole composition lives in the first screen. A small cross-hair cursor mark sits in the middle of the yellow circle.

## Shape, depth and texture
Cards are sharp rectangles with no shadow or border, sitting flat on the grey. The circle is a flat disc with no gradient. The only detail is the tick-mark scrubber and cursor.

## Motion
Not observed. The scrubber and cropped second card show the strip scrolls sideways.

## Why it does not look generic
There is no header, no project grid and no photo. The page is one typographic card and one geometric shape on a grey surface, and the navigation is a ruler.

## Steal this
- Lay content out as a horizontal strip of equal cards with the next card cropped by the viewport edge.
- Overlap a flat primary-colour shape with large type so the text crosses two backgrounds.
- Use a tick-mark ruler as the navigation control in place of a menu.

## Not for
Sites that need clear labels, search or many items; the strip hides what exists beyond the first two cards.
