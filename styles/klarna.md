# Klarna

**In one line:** a spare white payments homepage whose only loud element is a huge, dense headline, with the pink brand colour held back for a small sticker.

## Colour
White canvas and near-black ink (#0B051D, a purple-tinted black) for text, the nav sign-in pill and the main button. The signature Klarna pink #FFA8CD appears only on the floating "Get the app" card at the bottom right and in the secondary button style. A lavender strip at the top carries the scam warning. Body copy and secondary labels drop to a cool grey.

## Type
The headline is about 56px here (130px at the largest breakpoints), heavy, condensed and set with negative tracking so the letters nearly touch. Everything else is a clean regular sans around 16px, which makes the title look carved against it. Nav is small medium weight.

## Layout and rhythm
Two columns inside a roughly 1680px frame with 120px side margins. Left: headline, two lines of body and a pill button, anchored at the lower part of the column. Right: one tall rounded grey panel, about 800px square, holding a centred product cluster. Slider dots sit under it. A thin utility row (For shoppers, For business) and a wide search pill organise the top.

## Shape, depth and texture
Every control is a pill. The hero panel is a flat light grey with generous radius, no shadow, and the device photos float in its centre with plenty of empty margin. The pink app card overlaps the panel corner, rounded much more than the panel.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
Fintech heroes usually lead with gradients and phone mockups. Klarna uses a custom heavy display face and large flat white space, so typography does the branding and the colour is rationed to a single sticker.

## Steal this
- Pair one very heavy, tight display face with a plain regular body to get a brand voice without any decoration.
- Use a purple-tinted black instead of pure black for text and buttons so the neutrals belong to the brand.
- Reserve the signature colour for one floating element (a QR or app card) that overlaps another shape.

## Not for
Content-dense homepages that need many modules above the fold; this layout shows one idea at a time.
