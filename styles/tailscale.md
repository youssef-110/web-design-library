# Tailscale

**In one line:** a warm off-white networking page that uses one brick red to move you through five products.

## Colour
The canvas is warm bone (#F9F7F6) with charcoal text (#181717). Black (#1F1E1E) fills the announcement bar and the Get started button. The red (#D04841) fills the active product tab, the small PRODUCT eyebrow and the bullet markers, and tints the product window; each inactive tab carries its own small icon colour (blue, green, purple).

## Type
Inter at about 64px, regular weight, centred over two lines in charcoal, with an 18px grey paragraph. Top bar labels and small eyebrows use uppercase monospace. Section headings repeat Inter at about 48px.

## Layout and rhythm
An announcement bar, a white nav, then a centred hero: headline, subhead, a black pill button plus an underlined text link. Five equal tabs form a row, the active one a filled red card. Below it a wide white panel with rounded corners splits text left (eyebrow, title, bulleted list) and product screenshot right, with a floating status card overlapping its edge.

## Shape, depth and texture
Panels have generous radii, white on warm grey, with almost no shadow except the floating status card. Bullets are small red quarter circles and squares, a nod to the dotted logo.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
The red active tab sets the colour of the whole section, so switching product recolours the story. The geometric bullets echo the nine-dot logo, giving an enterprise page a toy-like detail.

## Steal this
- Make the active tab a solid accent card and every inactive one plain text with a tiny coloured icon.
- Overlap a small status card across the edge of the big product screenshot for depth without shadows.
- Replace list bullets with simple geometric shapes borrowed from the logo.

## Not for
Dense data dashboards; the large radii and wide gaps spend too much space.
