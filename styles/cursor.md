# Cursor

**In one line:** a warm, understated homepage for an AI coding agent, whose hero is a pair of app windows floating over a landscape painting.

## Colour
A warm off-white canvas (#F7F7F4) with a brown-black ink (#26251E), so every neutral leans yellow. The primary button is that ink, the secondary a stone grey (#E6E5E0). The painting behind the app is muted: dusty lilac sky, sand cliffs and soft green-blue water. Inside the windows, small amber buttons (Build, Continue) and green and red diff counters are the only chroma.

## Type
The headline is small, around 26px, in a regular-weight grotesque, on two left-aligned lines: it reads like a sentence and not a banner. The windows use small sans text with mono for code. The CURSOR wordmark is set in spaced capitals beside a hexagonal cube logo. Restraint in size makes the product visual dominant.

## Layout and rhythm
A 1300px container from x=310. A slim header (logo, centred nav, Sign in, Contact sales pill, black Download pill) is followed by about 120px of space, the headline, then two pill buttons ("Download for Windows" in ink, "Request a demo" in grey). Below sits a wide framed panel with the painting as its background, holding two overlapping windows: Cursor Desktop (task list, chat, spec document) and a smaller Cursor CLI terminal in front.

## Shape, depth and texture
The panel is edge-to-edge with the container, with the painting's visible brush texture. The windows have traffic-light dots, hairline borders and a faint shadow so they lift off the artwork. The terminal window is monospace with a bordered input. Pills are fully rounded.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
Developer tools are typically black with glow. Here a real oil painting is the backdrop, which makes the tool feel crafted and calm, and the small headline refuses to shout.

## Steal this
- Place product screenshots on top of a fine-art image (a painting or texture) so the UI gains depth with no gradient or shadow work.
- Show two overlapping windows (the GUI and the terminal) to communicate a multi-surface product in one frame.
- Keep the headline small and left-aligned, at about 26px, and give the visual the space.

## Not for
Brands that need a sharp, high-contrast feel; the warm paper tones and small type could look indecisive.
