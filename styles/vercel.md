# Vercel

**In one line:** an almost empty, near-white homepage for a deployment platform, with a lone black triangle as the whole illustration.

## Colour
Near-white canvas (#FAFAFA) with #171717 for text and the primary pill, #4D4D4D for secondary text and #EBEBEB for outlines. There is no brand hue anywhere in the first screen. The only dark mass is the logo triangle, which stands out because everything else is pale.

## Type
Geist Sans at about 64px, medium weight, with strong negative letter-spacing and two stacked left-aligned lines ("Agentic / Infrastructure"). Nav, buttons and the right-hand statements are about 15px regular sans. Mono is kept for code elsewhere on the site, not for the hero.

## Layout and rhythm
A 1400px container from x=260 to x=1660. The hero is a three-column composition on one horizontal band at mid-height: headline and two pill buttons on the left, the triangle centred, and three short lines stacked on the right ("For coding agents", "To ship apps and agents", "Automated by agents"). Above and below is wide empty space, with a row of six customer logos (Meta, DoorDash, OpenAI, SpaceX, The Weather Company, Polymarket) spread edge to edge along the bottom.

## Shape, depth and texture
Buttons are pills: black "Deploy now", white "Talk to sales" with a 1px ring, and in the nav "Get a Demo" and "Log In" as white pills and "Sign Up" black. The triangle is flat black with a soft white halo and a dark blurred shadow underneath, the only soft effect on the page.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
Where most platforms fill the hero with dashboards or code, this one has a single symbol, and the empty space around it makes the logo feel monumental.

## Steal this
- Make the hero a three-column band (headline left, one symbol centre, short statements right) and leave the rest of the viewport empty.
- Use strong negative letter-spacing on a medium-weight 64px headline so two short words look like a logo.
- Put customer logos in one evenly spread row pinned to the bottom edge of the first screen, with no heading above them.

## Not for
Products that need to explain a feature set in the first screen; the hero carries almost no information.
