# Brex

**In one line:** a confident, spacious homepage for corporate cards and finance software, led by a bold headline and a rendered card and phone.

## Colour
A white header over a very light grey hero (#F9F9F9) that warms to a faint peach glow at the bottom edge. Text is blue-black #15191E. The red-orange #FF3D00 is the accent: the Get started button in the nav, the button inside the email field, the See Brex in action link and the thin route lines across the hero image. A secondary grey at about 60% is used for the subline.

## Type
Inter at about 64px, semibold, set on two left-aligned lines with tight leading ("Finance built for / speed and control."). The subline is about 20px in mid grey, and the links and nav are about 15px medium. The weight of the headline against the airy page gives a strong first impression, with nothing else competing.

## Layout and rhythm
Header at the top with logo left, nav next to it, and Sign in, See a demo and an orange Get started on the right. The hero is a split composition: text column on the left from x=192, vertically centred, with about 350px of empty space above the headline; the right half is an oversized photographic render that bleeds off the right and bottom edges. The email field with its docked orange button sits below the subline, and a play-icon text link below that.

## Shape, depth and texture
The image is a 3D-looking still life: a black Mastercard-style Brex card lying on a rounded pale slab next to an iPhone showing a wallet screen, all tilted at a steep angle. Thin orange lines trace curved paths and a dot across the slab, and a field of small orange crosses sits on one side. Buttons are small rounded rectangles with no shadow; the field has a hairline border.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
The hero shows the physical card and the app together on one object, with orange lines that suggest routing and control, so the page leads with a tangible product rather than a dashboard.

## Steal this
- Bleed a tilted, oversized product render off the right and bottom edges of the hero to create depth without any shadow styling.
- Run a hairline accent-colour path through a hero image to tie the photograph to the UI colour.
- Dock the signup button inside the email field and add an accent-coloured text link with a play icon under it as a secondary action.

## Not for
Pages with no physical or rendered product to show; the layout depends on a strong hero image.
