# COLLINS

**In one line:** a brand strategy and design studio whose homepage is almost empty on purpose: a sentence, a row of awards, and the first glimpse of a film.

## Colour
Warm off-white (#F8F8F7) with a very dark brown-black (#140700) for all ink and the logo. The only other tone on the first screen is the near-black (#08080A) of the media panel at the bottom. No accent colour appears; interface states are white and dark pills.

## Type
The headline is a graceful old-style serif at about 72px, regular weight, centred in a single line with a full stop. It has a bookish, high-contrast feel with long descenders. The logo, nav and award captions are a geometric grotesque: the wordmark in wide spaced capitals, award labels at 12px. The scale jump from 72px to 12px is the hierarchy.

## Layout and rhythm
A 1240px content frame. Logo top left and a two-line menu glyph top right in an almost empty header. Then about 400px of blank page above the headline and another 250px below it before an eyebrow line ("8x Agency of the Year") and two rows of four laurel-framed award badges. The dark media panel starts around 970px down and is only 995px wide, centred, with rounded top corners, so it feels like a cinema screen opening.

## Shape, depth and texture
Flat, shadowless, and no borders. The only ornamental details are the tiny laurel branches around each award label. The dark panel's radius softens the otherwise strict composition.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
Agencies usually showcase a grid of work immediately. COLLINS spends the first viewport on one claim and silence, which acts as the proof of confidence, then credits itself with awards in a quiet cluster.

## Steal this
- Dedicate the first screen to a single serif sentence with vast blank space around it and push the work below the fold.
- Present awards as a compact grid of laurel-framed text labels, centred beneath the headline.
- Reveal media in a dark rounded panel narrower than the text frame, so it reads as a screening.

## Not for
Products that need immediate explanation or conversion, since the first screen carries no feature or button.
