# Uber

**In one line:** a service hub that leads with a working booking form and a hotel search, then lists every product as a card.

## Colour
Black canvas (#000000) with white text, and dark grey (#292929) cards. Inputs and the main buttons are white. A green (#58D89C family) appears as the outline on the Uber Eats photos. Alternating sections turn white, so the page flips between black and white bands. Photography supplies colour.

## Type
A bold geometric grotesque for headings ("Go anywhere", "Explore what you can do with Uber", "How to request a ride"), about 36px, in white, left aligned. Card titles are small and bold, with body text at about 14px.

## Layout and rhythm
Header: logo left, a row of dark pill chips (Ride, Earn, Travel, Hotels, Business, Uber Eats, About), then EN, Help, Log in and a white Sign up pill. A wide beach photo strip carries "Introducing Hotels on Uber" with a white pill search bar (destinations, dates, travellers, black Search button) overlapping its bottom edge. Below, a two-column hero: city selector, "Go anywhere", Pickup now pill, two stacked inputs and See prices at left, a 300px square hotel photo with an "Explore Hotels" overlay at right. A grid of six dark cards follows, then paired text and image rows (left text, right image), a three-step carousel, and green-outlined food photos on a white band, and finally app download cards with QR codes in a white band and a black footer.

## Shape, depth and texture
Cards are 8px dark grey rectangles with small icons or illustrations; inputs are white with small radii; chips and buttons are pills. No shadows. Photos have rounded corners; some are cropped into wide banners with rounded ends.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
The main action is on the page, not behind a button: you can type a pickup and drop-off at once. The hotel search bar overlaps the photo so the new product gets the top slot.

## Steal this
- Put the actual input form in the hero, with a secondary promo strip above it.
- Introduce a new product with a wide photo strip and a pill search bar that overlaps its lower edge.
- Use a row of small dark pill chips as the nav so every product line is visible at once.

## Not for
Brands that need editorial calm or heavy storytelling; the page is a tool panel and a card list.
