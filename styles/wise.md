# Wise

**In one line:** a money-transfer homepage that shouts in tightly stacked capitals on a solid lime field and puts a live calculator directly under the headline.

## Colour
The lime (#9FE870) is not an accent here, it is the page background for the entire first screen. White appears only inside the calculator card, which makes the product the single bright object on the screen. The primary button is dark green with lime text, an inversion of the page.

## Type
The headline is set in capitals at a heavy 900 weight, centred over two lines at roughly 90px, with tracking pulled so tight the letters nearly touch. Everything else, nav and subhead, is a quiet medium-weight sans.

## Layout and rhythm
Strictly centred single column inside a wide margin: nav row, headline, three-line subhead, one pill button, then the calculator card rising from the bottom edge so it is cut by the fold.

## Shape, depth and texture
The calculator card is a narrow, tall rounded panel (about 40px radius) with a dark photographic header of green farmland carrying a lime headline, then a white sheet with grey pill selectors and a pale blue info strip.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
Most fintech heroes show a hero photo or phone. Wise shows the working tool itself, a fee calculator, beneath type big enough to be a poster. Green on green, with no black anywhere in the hero, makes the palette feel like one brand colour with depth rather than an accent on white.

## Steal this
- Make the background the brand colour and set all text in a very dark shade of the same hue, so you get contrast without a second colour.
- Put the real product control (a calculator, a form) directly under the headline and let it break the fold, so the first scroll is already using the product.
- Set numbers in your headline face so data and message share one voice.

## Not for
Brands that need a calm, premium tone.
