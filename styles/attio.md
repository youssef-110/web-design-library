# Attio

**In one line:** a clean white homepage for a CRM, centred and calm, leading straight into a large screenshot of its AI-first home screen.

## Colour
White canvas, #2E3238 text, an ink fill (#202124) for the main buttons and cool grey borders (#D3D8DF). A grey subtitle uses a lighter slate tone. A pale blue-lilac wash fades in behind the top of the screenshot. Colour inside the mockup is minimal: a blue send button, a few status dots in amber, green and grey.

## Type
The headline is about 69px, medium weight in the Inter Display cut, with tight negative tracking, centred on one line. A two-line grey subtitle at about 19px and small 15px button labels follow. The display cut gives tight, crisp letterforms that read as more refined than default Inter.

## Layout and rhythm
A black announcement bar on top, then a white header with the wordmark left, nav next to it and Sign in plus a dark Start for free right. The hero is fully centred: a small pill link with an arrow, the headline, subtitle, a pair of buttons (outlined Talk to sales, dark Start for free), then the screenshot, which is wider than the text column (about 1440px) and cropped by the fold. The cookie banner and chat bubble sit over the corners.

## Shape, depth and texture
The screenshot is placed in a window frame with red, yellow and green dots and generous rounded corners. Inside, the home screen shows a sidebar of icon nav items, a "Good morning" greeting, a large prompt box with a blue send button and suggestion chips, and a meetings list with strikethrough past items. Surfaces use hairline borders and very light grey fills; buttons are 10px-radius rectangles with no shadow.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
The screenshot is not a table of records but an AI prompt input ("How do I win my deal with...?"), which tells the visitor immediately that this CRM is conversational.

## Steal this
- Choose the screenshot state that explains your idea (a prompt box, not a data grid), and enlarge it beyond the text column width.
- Pair an outlined and a dark button at 10px radius so two actions look equal in shape, unequal in weight.
- Use the display optical cut of your typeface with tight tracking for headlines over 60px.

## Not for
Products whose appeal is dense data or customisation; the airy, centred layout hides complexity.
