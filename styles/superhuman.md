# Superhuman

**In one line:** a dramatic, photographic homepage for an AI productivity suite, with a portrait at dusk and floating product fragments.

## Colour
Above the photo, a white header and a lavender strip (#E8E0FF) holding a link to the mail product. The hero is a full-bleed twilight photo in blue, violet and dark plum with a pale pink glow, overlaid with white text. The primary button is deep navy (#1B1938) with an indigo ring and a lighter lavender arrow tile. Text on white is a warm charcoal (#292827). The floating cards are translucent purple glass.

## Type
The headline is about 64px at regular weight in white, centred on two lines ("Superpowers, / everywhere you work"), with a 26px subline below. Product fragments use small sans text at 14 to 16px. The wordmark is set in bold spaced capitals next to a rounded-square badge with a figure mark. Large, light white type over a soft photograph reads as calm and premium.

## Layout and rhythm
The photo begins directly under the header strips and fills the full width. Centred headline, subline and a single CTA ("Get Superhuman" with an arrow tile) occupy the top third; below, the woman's profile fills the centre. Around her, glass panels are arranged asymmetrically: an AI chat card at left, a vertical icon dock beside it, an inbox list at lower left, a docs editor at right with a floating "Tailor this language for executives" chip, and a Grammarly chip at lower right.

## Shape, depth and texture
Panels are semi-transparent with blurred backgrounds, thin light borders and rounded corners of about 16px, so the photograph shows through them. Chips are fully rounded. The button has a thick dark-navy fill with a 2px indigo outline, and the arrow sits in its own lighter rounded square. The photograph has grain and soft focus at the lower edge.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
Instead of a screenshot on a gradient, the hero puts a real person in the scene and attaches the product's features to her as floating thoughts, which humanises an AI tool.

## Steal this
- Scatter four to six translucent glass cards around a photographed subject, each showing a different feature, instead of one large screenshot.
- Build a button with a dark fill, a coloured ring and an inner contrasting arrow tile to make a single CTA feel premium.
- Add a thin lavender strip under the header for a secondary link so the main hero stays uncluttered.

## Not for
Brands with no budget for photography; the effect depends on a strong, well-graded portrait.
