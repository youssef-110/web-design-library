# Sweetgreen

**In one line:** a salad brand that sells with one huge, appetising overhead photo and treats every other element as quiet.

## Colour
The nav bar is a cream (#F4F3E7) with deep forest green (#00473C) type and wordmark. The hero is almost entirely photography: dark walnut brown on the left, a speckled stoneware plate, roasted sweet potato, broccoli, glazed chicken and a lemon wedge. One acid lime (#E6FF55) pill with green text, ORDER NOW, is the only flat colour in the hero, and a dark green ORDER pill sits in the nav.

## Type
The headline "Deeply Layered Flavor" is a light (300) weight sans at about 64px in cream, in title case with tight tracking, left aligned at the bottom of the image.

## Layout and rhythm
A narrow accessibility strip, then a cream nav in three zones: links at left (Our Menu, Our Mission, Fundraising), the centred wordmark, and links with the green ORDER pill at the right, all on one hairline-separated row about 70px tall. The photo fills the full width and the rest of the viewport. The text group is placed at the bottom left, with 280px side margin, overlapping the plate edge.

## Shape, depth and texture
The photo is shot from directly above on wood with strong natural light and visible texture on the food and plate, so it feels tactile. Buttons are small pills.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
The photograph is shot as a still life, with the plate cropped by the frame, and the lettering lays over it in a light weight rather than a bold one. The lime pill echoes herbs and citrus on the plate. The cream bar with a centred wordmark reads like a restaurant menu header.

## Steal this
- Make a full-bleed overhead product photograph the whole hero and set a light-weight headline low left over a quiet area of the image.
- Use one acid-coloured pill with dark brand-colour text as the only flat colour over a photo.
- Put the logo in the centre of a three-zone uppercase nav with the action at the right edge.

## Not for
Products that cannot be photographed beautifully from above.
