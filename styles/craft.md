# Craft

**In one line:** a cheerful notes-app homepage framed like a postcard: a blue sky, torn paper collage and a colourful app window.

## Colour
The page is a warm cream (#FCF9F7) with one big rounded panel inside it, filled with a sky-blue gradient (#94CFEC at the top to #A1D6EF). Text and buttons are near-black. The collage adds a periwinkle blob, a grey and a black torn shape, white zigzag paper and a yellow legal pad. In the app window, document cards are pink, white, yellow, green and blue. The orange-red #FF4500 is declared as the accent.

## Type
The headline is a serif at about 64px in regular weight, centred on two lines with tight leading ("Your space for notes, tasks, and big ideas"), in a very dark black over the sky. The nav and buttons use a clean sans at about 15px, and the wordmark is a chunky, rounded display set in capitals. The serif headline against a sans UI gives a bookish warmth.

## Layout and rhythm
A hero panel with a 20px page margin and large rounded corners. A floating translucent pill nav is centred at the top (logo, six links, Log in, black Try Craft Free pill). Headline and a single translucent pill CTA are centred about a third of the way down. At the bottom edge, the app window (traffic lights, sidebar, grid of colourful doc cards) sits in front of the collage and a large outlined ghost "CRAFT" in thin strokes spans the middle.

## Shape, depth and texture
Clouds are dithered halftone shapes, the torn paper shapes have rough white edges, and the legal pad has ruled lines and a red margin. The nav and CTA pills use soft translucent backgrounds with gentle shadows. The app window's card colours are light pastels with bold coloured top borders.

## Motion
Not observed.

## Why it does not look generic
A note-taking app is shown as a collage of paper and sky, which makes the product feel tactile and personal. The torn edges and halftone clouds feel handmade.

## Steal this
- Put a hero inside one large rounded panel with a margin of cream around it so it looks like a card, not a full-bleed banner.
- Layer the product window over torn-paper shapes and a ghosted outline of your wordmark to create depth with flat assets.
- Use a dithered or halftone texture on clouds or gradients to make a digital sky look printed.

## Not for
Serious or regulated products; the playfulness would undercut trust.
