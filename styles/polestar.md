# Polestar

**In one line:** an electric car brand that borrows gallery restraint: one object, one grey room, a few plain words.

## Colour
Almost no colour on the first screen. The ground is a cool, slightly green grey (#DEE0DF) that fades to white at the edges, the car is gloss black with one thin red tail light, and the text is black plus a mid grey. A black announcement bar caps the page. The orange #F06E00 appears in the stylesheet as an accent but is not visible here, so the red light bar on the car is the only hot point.

## Type
Polestar Unica, a neo-grotesque close to Helvetica. The heading, about 30px, has two tones: the product name "Polestar 3" in black and the offer line beneath in mid grey, both tightly leaded with negative tracking. Body is 16px black, nav is 16px regular. Sizes stay modest; nothing is shouted.

## Layout and rhythm
A 160px left margin holds logo, headline, paragraph and two buttons, all flush left and stacked in the upper third. The car occupies the right 60%, sitting low and cropped by the right edge, in three-quarter rear view. The nav is a single right-aligned row with seven plain text links and two icons. A pause control sits at the lower right.

## Shape, depth and texture
Buttons are 1px black outlined rectangles with an arrow, no fill, no radius. A long soft cast shadow from the car on the grey floor is the only depth. The paint reflects the studio like a mirror.

## Motion
The hero is a video or slideshow, signalled by the circular pause control at the bottom right.

## Why it does not look generic
Car sites often use dramatic outdoor action. Polestar uses a showroom blankness and square, outlined UI so the brand reads as Scandinavian industrial design instead of adventure.

## Steal this
- Stack a headline in two tones: product name in black, offer or claim in mid grey, to get hierarchy without size changes.
- Use outlined square buttons with arrows over a pale photographic ground to avoid colour clashes.
- Crop the product off the edge and keep the opposite half empty so a long shadow becomes the composition.

## Not for
Brands that need warmth or humour; the surface is deliberately cold.
