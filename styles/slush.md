# Slush

**In one line:** a crypto wallet that sells itself like a sticker book, with one gigantic word and bright colour tiles.

## Colour
The page sits on black and the main panels are rounded boxes: pale blue (#DCEEFF) for the hero, white for most feature sections, then tiles in yellow (#FFD731), blue (#4DA2FF), orange (#FB4903), lilac (#E9CCFF) and mint green. A lilac ticker strip (#E9CCFF) runs across the top. Black is used for headline type and the Launch App button.

## Type
The hero word "SLUSH" is set in an ultra-condensed black face, roughly 360px tall at 1280 width and about half the viewport width, with the tagline "Your money. Unstuck." in a regular grotesque below. Feature headlines ("EXPLORE DEFI OPPORTUNITIES", "DON'T BELIEVE US?") are the same condensed face, in black caps, often slanted to italic. Body copy is small and plain.

## Layout and rhythm
The first panel is a rounded pale-blue box: a circular logo and a row of outlined pill links (Get started, Card, DeFi, Security, Guides, Download, a plus sign) with a black Launch App pill at right, the huge word centred, a 3D blue textured tube snaking behind it, and sticker illustrations (rocket, coin, yellow smiley, purple wallet) pinned to the letters. Two outlined pills sit under the tagline. Following sections are white rounded boxes stacked on the black page, each with a centred sentence-case title and alternating image and condensed headline rows. A band of repeating "GET SLUSH" tiles in different colours, a grid of coloured partner icons on black, and a green footer panel with social tiles end it.

## Shape, depth and texture
Large rounded panels (about 28px), outlined pills for secondary buttons and square-ish bevelled tiles. The blue tube has a grainy, felt-like 3D texture; the stickers have thick black outlines.

## Motion
Not observed. The top ticker strip repeats its message edge to edge.

## Why it does not look generic
Finance brands usually show security and charts. Here the hero is a giant word with toy-like stickers, and the colour tiles repeat the same four hues, so the brand feels like a game.

## Steal this
- Make one word of the hero fill half the viewport width in an ultra-condensed face, and pin small outlined stickers to its edges.
- Stack white rounded panels on a black page so each section reads as a separate card.
- Repeat a coloured tile band of the call to action ("GET SLUSH") as a divider before the footer.

## Not for
Regulated or conservative finance, and any audience that needs reassurance before action; the toy tone could undermine trust.
