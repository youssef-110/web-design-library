# Shopify

**In one line:** a commerce platform homepage that opens like a film trailer and then moves through dark, colour-washed panels.

## Colour
The page is a near-black teal (#02090A) that shifts to deep green (#041E18) in the middle sections and to a saturated indigo purple (#2C007F) for the Sidekick AI section, then back to dark. Text is white, with grey (#E0E0E0) for supporting copy and for the first half of two-tone headlines. Product screenshots and merchant photos supply bright colour.

## Type
A neutral grotesque set in a light weight with tight tracking. The hero headline "Be the next household name" is white, about 70px at 1280 width and left aligned over two lines. The next heading ("Sell everywhere people shop. Online and in person. Across AI and on social.") is two-tone: grey for the first sentences, white for the emphasised phrases. Section titles later are smaller, regular weight and left aligned.

## Layout and rhythm
A transparent nav over the video: logo, four links, an outlined "Fall '26 Edition" pill, Log in and a white "Start for free" pill. The hero is a full-bleed video of a person laughing into their hand, with a headline, two lines of support text and two pills (solid white, and outlined "Start with your agent") on a 1000px left-aligned column. The video's lower edge is cut by a large rounded dark panel. Below, each section follows a pattern: left-aligned heading, then a row of three rounded cards with a caption underneath, with one full-width card with a phone cluster. Further down are a green globe image with two big statistics, a logo-and-photo testimonial, an app-icon wall, and a footer.

## Shape, depth and texture
Pills for buttons; large rounded corners on panels and cards; no hard borders. Backgrounds carry soft green or purple glow and starfield-like dots. Imagery mixes video, merchant photos, UI screens and a 3D globe.

## Motion
Not observed. The hero is video.

## Why it does not look generic
It treats a store builder like a launch event: video first, dark colour fields that change by section, and headlines in a light weight instead of bold. Merchant photos stand in for stock illustrations.

## Steal this
- Open with a full-bleed video and cut its bottom edge with a rounded panel that starts the content.
- Colour each section's background from its subject (green for the global story, purple for the AI feature).
- Write headlines in two greys so the emphasised phrase reads without bold weight.

## Not for
Small shops or content sites where heavy video and dark colour wash would hurt load time and legibility.
